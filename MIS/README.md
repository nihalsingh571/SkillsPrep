## 🎯 What is an MIS Executive?

An **MIS (Management Information System) Executive** is the backbone of data reporting in any organization. They collect, organize, analyze, and present business data to help management make informed decisions.

**You are the bridge between raw data and business decisions.**

## 💼 MIS Executive — Job Responsibilities

1. Prepare and distribute **daily/weekly/monthly reports** to stakeholders.
2. **Collect data** from multiple sources (ERP, CRM, trackers, field teams).
3. **Clean and validate data** — remove duplicates, fix errors.
4. Build and maintain **Excel dashboards** and **Power BI reports**.
5. Track **KPIs and SLAs** — highlight deviations and exceptions.
6. **Automate repetitive tasks** using Macros/VBA.
7. **Present data insights** to managers and senior leadership.
8. Maintain **process trackers, headcount reports, productivity reports**.
9. Coordinate with multiple departments (Operations, HR, Finance, Sales).

## ⚡ Key Interview Topics That Are Always Asked

1. What is VLOOKUP? What are its limitations?
2. Difference between VLOOKUP and INDEX/MATCH
3. What is a Pivot Table? How do you create one?
4. How do you remove duplicates in Excel?
5. What is SUMIF vs SUMIFS?
6. What is Power BI? What is a DAX formula?
7. What is a KPI? Give an example.
8. How do you handle missing data in a report?
9. What is conditional formatting? Where do you use it?
10. Tell me about a dashboard you created.
