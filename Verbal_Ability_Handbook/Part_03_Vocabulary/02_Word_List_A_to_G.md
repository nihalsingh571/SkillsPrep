# Part 3: Complete Vocabulary (A to G)

## 📖 Chapter Overview
This section covers the most critical high-frequency words from A to G tested in TCS NQT, Infosys, Accenture, and other major placement exams. We dive deep into the roots, contextual usage, and memory tricks.

## 🏆 Part 1: Deep Dive Placement Vocabulary

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Never memorize words in isolation. Always understand their tone (positive/negative) to easily eliminate options in synonym/antonym questions.

### 1. **Aberrant**
- **Pronunciation:** /əˈber.ənt/
- **Part of Speech:** Adjective
- **Meaning (Primary):** Departing from an accepted standard.
- **Synonyms:** Deviant, abnormal, atypical, anomalous.
- **Antonyms:** Normal, typical, customary.
- **Example:** His aberrant behavior during the technical interview raised red flags.
- **Root Analysis:** Latin *ab-* (away from) + *errare* (to wander).
> [!TIP]
> **Memory Trick:** "A-bear-ant" -> An ant acting like a bear is definitely abnormal!
- **Company Context:** Frequently asked in **TCS NQT**.

### 2. **Abeyance**
- **Pronunciation:** /əˈbeɪ.əns/
- **Part of Speech:** Noun
- **Meaning:** A state of temporary disuse or suspension.
- **Synonyms:** Suspension, remission, reserve, dormancy.
- **Antonyms:** Continuation, action, revival.
- **Example:** The new hiring policy is currently in abeyance pending board approval.
> [!TIP]
> **Memory Trick:** "Obey-and-wait" -> You are in a state of waiting (suspension).
- **Company Context:** Tested heavily in **Infosys** sentence completion.

### 3. **Abscond**
- **Pronunciation:** /əbˈskɒnd/
- **Part of Speech:** Verb
- **Meaning:** Leave hurriedly and secretly, typically to avoid detection.
- **Synonyms:** Flee, escape, bolt, decamp.
- **Antonyms:** Remain, abide, endure.
- **Example:** The corrupt manager absconded with the company's funds.
> [!TIP]
> **Memory Trick:** "Absence in a second" -> He ran away instantly.
- **Company Context:** Common in **Accenture** Error Detection.

### 4. **Accolade**
- **Pronunciation:** /ˈæk.ə.leɪd/
- **Part of Speech:** Noun
- **Meaning:** An award or privilege granted as a special honor.
- **Synonyms:** Honor, recognition, privilege, award.
- **Antonyms:** Criticism, rebuke, admonishment.
- **Example:** The CEO received the highest accolade for her leadership.
> [!TIP]
> **Memory Trick:** "A-cool-aid" -> If you do great, you get a cool drink as a reward!
- **Company Context:** **Cognizant GenC**.

### 5. **Acrimonious**
- **Pronunciation:** /ˌæk.rɪˈməʊ.ni.əs/
- **Part of Speech:** Adjective
- **Meaning:** Angry and bitter (typically of speech or a debate).
- **Synonyms:** Bitter, rancorous, caustic, acerbic.
- **Antonyms:** Amicable, cordial, pleasant.
- **Example:** The two departments had an acrimonious dispute over the budget.
> [!TIP]
> **Memory Trick:** "A-crime-on-us" -> It leaves a bitter and angry feeling.
- **Company Context:** **Wipro NLTH**.

## 🧠 Practice Questions

---
**Q1. Choose the correct synonym for the word: ABERRANT**

(A) Conventional  
(B) Deviant  
(C) Punctual  
(D) Diligent  

**✅ Answer: (B)**

**📖 Explanation:** The word 'Aberrant' means departing from an accepted standard (abnormal). 
**❌ Why (A) is wrong:** 'Conventional' is an antonym.  
**❌ Why (C) is wrong:** 'Punctual' means on time (unrelated).  
**❌ Why (D) is wrong:** 'Diligent' means hardworking.

**📏 Rule:** Contextual Vocabulary Match  
**⚡ Shortcut:** A-bear-ant -> abnormal -> Deviant.  
**📊 Difficulty:** Medium  
**🏢 Company:** TCS NQT
