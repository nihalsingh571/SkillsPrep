# Part 3: Complete Vocabulary (H to P)

## 📖 Chapter Overview
This section tackles the heavily tested H to P vocabulary block. Words like *Hypocritical*, *Inevitable*, and *Pragmatic* form the core of Reading Comprehension passages in exams like TCS and Infosys.

## 🏆 Part 1: Deep Dive Placement Vocabulary

> [!WARNING]
> Be careful with words starting with 'In-'. While usually a negative prefix (e.g., *Incapable*), in words like *Invaluable* or *Inflammable*, it means highly valuable or highly flammable!

### 1. **Harangue**
- **Pronunciation:** /həˈræŋ/
- **Part of Speech:** Noun / Verb
- **Meaning:** A lengthy and aggressive speech.
- **Synonyms:** Tirade, diatribe, lecture, rant.
- **Antonyms:** Panegyric, tribute, eulogy.
- **Example:** The manager delivered a harangue to the team about their missed deadlines.
> [!TIP]
> **Memory Trick:** "Har-anger" -> A speech delivered in intense anger!
- **Company Context:** **Infosys** Reading Comprehension.

### 2. **Harbinger**
- **Pronunciation:** /ˈhɑː.bɪn.dʒər/
- **Part of Speech:** Noun
- **Meaning:** A person or thing that announces or signals the approach of another.
- **Synonyms:** Herald, sign, indicator, precursor.
- **Antonyms:** Follower, aftermath.
- **Example:** The sudden drop in stock prices was a harbinger of the coming recession.
> [!TIP]
> **Memory Trick:** "Her-bringer" -> She brings the news of what is coming.
- **Company Context:** **TCS NQT** synonym matching.

### 3. **Haughty**
- **Pronunciation:** /ˈhɔː.ti/
- **Part of Speech:** Adjective
- **Meaning:** Arrogantly superior and disdainful.
- **Synonyms:** Arrogant, snobbish, conceited, pompous.
- **Antonyms:** Humble, modest, unassuming.
- **Example:** The haughty executive refused to speak with the junior interns.
> [!TIP]
> **Memory Trick:** "High-tea" -> People who only drink high-tea can act snobbish and superior.
- **Company Context:** **Accenture** Fill in the Blanks.

### 4. **Hegemony**
- **Pronunciation:** /hɪˈdʒem.ə.ni/
- **Part of Speech:** Noun
- **Meaning:** Leadership or dominance, especially by one country or social group over others.
- **Synonyms:** Dominance, control, supremacy.
- **Antonyms:** Subordination, submission.
- **Example:** The tech giant's hegemony in the search engine market remains unchallenged.
> [!TIP]
> **Memory Trick:** "Huge-money" -> If you have huge money, you have dominance and control.
- **Company Context:** **Cognizant GenC**.

### 5. **Heretical**
- **Pronunciation:** /həˈret.ɪ.kəl/
- **Part of Speech:** Adjective
- **Meaning:** Holding an opinion at odds with what is generally accepted.
- **Synonyms:** Unorthodox, dissenting, iconoclastic.
- **Antonyms:** Orthodox, conformist, traditional.
- **Example:** Suggesting that the project should be scrapped was considered heretical by the founders.
> [!TIP]
> **Memory Trick:** "Here-trick" -> Suggesting a new trick that goes against the old ways.
- **Company Context:** **Wipro NLTH**.

## 🧠 Practice Questions

**Q1. Choose the ANTONYM for the word: HAUGHTY**

(A) Arrogant  
(B) Humble  
(C) Wealthy  
(D) Intelligent  

**✅ Answer: (B)**

**📖 Explanation:** The word 'Haughty' means arrogantly superior. The exact opposite is humble.
**❌ Why (A) is wrong:** 'Arrogant' is a synonym.  
**❌ Why (C) is wrong:** Wealthy refers to money, not attitude.  
**❌ Why (D) is wrong:** Intelligent is completely unrelated.

**📏 Rule:** Tone Elimination (Haughty is negative tone, we need a positive tone antonym).  
**⚡ Shortcut:** High-tea -> Snob. Antonym -> Down to earth / Humble.  
**📊 Difficulty:** Easy  
**🏢 Company:** Accenture
