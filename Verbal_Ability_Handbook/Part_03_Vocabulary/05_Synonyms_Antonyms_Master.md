# Synonyms & Antonyms Master Guide

## 📖 Chapter Overview
Synonyms and Antonyms form the core of 30-40% of the Verbal Ability section in IT Placements. This guide provides the most frequently tested pairs, reveals tricky traps, and introduces "Contronyms"—the ultimate placement exam trap.

## 🧠 Section 1: Contronyms (The Ultimate Trap)
> [!CAUTION]
> A **Contronym** (or auto-antonym) is a word that can be its own opposite depending on the context! Examiners LOVE these words because they confuse students.

| Contronym | Meaning 1 (Positive/Action) | Meaning 2 (Negative/Inaction) | Example of Trap |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Sanction** | To approve (The UN sanctioned the treaty) | To boycott/punish (The UN sanctioned the nation) | Context determines if it's approval or punishment! |
| **Cleave** | To stick tightly to (Cleave to your beliefs) | To split apart (Cleave the wood in two) | Look at the preposition! "Cleave TO" = stick. |
| **Oversight** | Careful supervision (Project oversight) | An accidental error (A terrible oversight) | "Oversight committee" vs "It was an oversight." |
| **Fast** | Moving quickly (A fast runner) | Unmoving, stuck (Stuck fast in the mud) | Fast colors = colors that don't wash away. |
| **Dust** | To add fine particles (Dust the cake with sugar) | To remove fine particles (Dust the shelves) | Action direction changes entirely. |

## 🧩 Section 2: Most Tricky Synonym Pairs
These words look incredibly different but mean the exact same thing.

| Word 1 | Word 2 (Synonym) | Meaning | Memory Trick |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Paucity** | **Dearth** | Scarcity / Lack of something | Paucity (Pause-city: no growth), Dearth (Death: gone). |
| **Garrulous** | **Loquacious** | Very talkative | Garrulous (Gargle words), Loqu (Root for speech). |
| **Ephemeral** | **Transient** | Short-lived | Ephemeral (E-fem-eral: fleeting), Transit (moves fast).|
| **Esoteric** | **Abstruse** | Hard to understand | Eso (Inner circle), Ab-struse (Abstract truth). |
| **Belligerent**| **Pugnacious** | Aggressive, eager to fight | Bell (War), Pug (Fist/boxer). |

## ⚔️ Section 3: Most Tricky Antonym Pairs
These words look like they should be synonyms, but they are exact opposites!

| Word | Tricky Antonym | Why they look similar |
|---|---|---|
| **Amiable** (Friendly) | **Inimical** (Hostile) | Both end in a soft suffix, sound pleasant. |
| **Enervate** (Exhaust) | **Invigorate** (Energize)| Enervate sounds like "Energy", but means losing it! |
| **Inflammable** (Burns) | **Non-flammable** (Safe)| "In" usually means not, but here it means *into flames*. |
| **Frugal** (Thrifty) | **Prodigal** (Wasteful) | Both end in -gal, sound like economic terms. |

## 🧠 Practice Questions
---
**Q1. The CEO’s _____ decision saved the company from bankruptcy.**

(A) Frivolous  
(B) Sagacious  
(C) Inept  
(D) Lethargic  

**✅ Answer: (B)**

**📖 Explanation:** The sentence has a positive tone ("saved the company"). 'Sagacious' means wise and showing good judgment.  
**❌ Why (A) is wrong:** Frivolous means silly/pointless (Negative).  
**❌ Why (C) is wrong:** Inept means clumsy/unskilled (Negative).  
**❌ Why (D) is wrong:** Lethargic means lazy (Negative).

**📏 Rule:** Sentence Tone Mapping.  
**⚡ Shortcut:** Eliminate all negative words. Sagacious (Sage = wise) is the only positive choice.  
**📊 Difficulty:** Medium  
**🏢 Company:** TCS NQT
