# Root Words, Prefixes & Suffixes

## 📖 Chapter Overview
Memorizing 5000 words is impossible. Memorizing 50 roots allows you to dynamically decode 50,000 words! This is the secret weapon used by 99th percentile scorers.

## 🛠️ The DECODER Method
When you see an unknown word in an exam, use the **DECODER** method:
1. **Divide**: Split the word into Prefix + Root + Suffix. (e.g., *In-cred-ible*)
2. **Extract**: Find the meaning of the core Root. (*Cred* = Believe)
3. **Context**: Add the Prefix/Suffix meaning. (*In* = Not, *ible* = able to be)
4. **Determine**: Combine them! (*Incredible* = Not able to be believed = Amazing).

## 🏗️ Section 2: Essential Prefixes (The Word Modifiers)
Prefixes change the direction or tone of the root.

| Prefix | Meaning | Examples | Impact on Tone |
|---|---|---|---|
| **A-, An-** | Without, lacking | Amoral, apathy, anemic | Reverses to Negative |
| **Anti-** | Against, opposite | Antidote, antisocial | Reverses to Negative |
| **Circum-** | Around | Circumference, circumvent | Neutral (Spatial) |
| **Co-, Com-**| With, together | Coexist, companion, compile| Unifying |
| **De-** | Down, off, away from| Descend, degrade, deduct | Negative / Decreasing |
| **In-, Im-** | Not (usually) | Impossible, inactive, illogical| Reverses to Negative |
| **Pre-** | Before | Precede, predict | Time indicator |

## 🏁 Section 3: Essential Suffixes (The Grammar Deciders)
Suffixes usually don't change the meaning, they change the **Part of Speech**. This is crucial for Fill-in-the-Blanks!

| Suffix | Part of Speech | Meaning | Examples |
|---|---|---|---|
| **-tion, -sion** | Noun | State of being | Action, confusion |
| **-ity, -ty** | Noun | Quality of | Creativity, cruelty |
| **-ous, -ious** | Adjective | Possessing the quality of | Joyous, religious |
| **-able, -ible**| Adjective | Capable of | Solvable, incredible |
| **-ify, -fy** | Verb | To make or become | Justify, beautify |
| **-ly** | Adverb | In what manner | Quickly, silently |

## 🧠 Practice Questions: The Decoder Method

---
**Q1. Without knowing the exact definition, decode the word "SOMNAMBULIST".**

(A) A person who studies animals  
(B) A person who walks in their sleep  
(C) A person who fears darkness  
(D) A person who collects coins  

**✅ Answer: (B)**

**📖 Explanation:** 
- Root 1: *Somn* = Sleep (Think: Insomnia = no sleep).
- Root 2: *Ambul* = Walk (Think: Ambulance = moving hospital).
- Suffix: *Ist* = A person who does.
- Combine: A person who walks in their sleep.

**📏 Rule:** Root Word Decoding  
**⚡ Shortcut:** Somn (sleep) + Ambul (walk).  
**📊 Difficulty:** Hard
