# Foreign Words in English

## 📖 Chapter Overview
Many placement exams (especially TCS NQT and Cognizant) feature fill-in-the-blank or synonym questions testing common foreign expressions adopted into English. Knowing these adds polish to your vocabulary and guarantees easy points.

## 🏛️ Section 1: Latin Expressions
Latin is the foundation of legal, academic, and professional English.

| Expression | Meaning | Example Usage |
|---|---|---|
| **Ad hoc** | Formed for a particular purpose | We formed an *ad hoc* committee to solve the bug. |
| **Bona fide** | Genuine; real (in good faith) | Ensure you are dealing with a *bona fide* recruitment agency. |
| **Per se** | By itself; inherently | It is not a bad design *per se*, but it doesn’t fit our requirements. |
| **Prima facie**| At first sight; based on first impression | *Prima facie*, the candidate seemed perfect for the role. |
| **Status quo** | The existing state of affairs | The disruptive startup aimed to change the *status quo* of the industry. |
| **Vice versa** | The other way around | He doesn't trust her, and *vice versa*. |
| **De facto** | In fact (whether by right or not) | English is the *de facto* language of software engineering. |
| **Alma mater** | The school/university one attended | IIT Delhi is his *alma mater*. |
| **Carpe diem** | Seize the day (make the most of present) | Her attitude towards the challenging project was *carpe diem*. |
| **Caveat emptor**| Let the buyer beware | Buying used laptops online is a classic case of *caveat emptor*. |

## 🥐 Section 2: French Expressions
French expressions are often used in art, diplomacy, and sophisticated corporate communication.

| Expression | Meaning | Example Usage |
|---|---|---|
| **En masse** | In a group; all together | The employees resigned *en masse* after the policy change. |
| **Faux pas** | A socially awkward or tactless act | Wearing shorts to the board meeting was a massive *faux pas*. |
| **Avant-garde** | Innovative; ahead of its time | Their UI design is highly *avant-garde*. |
| **Coup de grâce**| A final blow or finishing stroke | The leaked scandal was the *coup de grâce* for the CEO's career. |
| **Déjà vu** | The feeling of having experienced this before| Sitting in the same interview room gave me a sense of *déjà vu*. |
| **Rapport** | A close and harmonious relationship | Build good *rapport* with your team lead. |
| **Fait accompli**| A thing that has already happened (irreversible)| By the time we arrived, the merger was a *fait accompli*. |
| **Vis-à-vis** | In relation to; compared with | Our profit margins are high *vis-à-vis* our competitors. |

## 🥨 Section 3: German Expressions
German words often describe specific complex psychological or cultural states.

| Expression | Meaning | Example Usage |
|---|---|---|
| **Angst** | Deep anxiety or dread | The stock market crash caused widespread *angst* among investors. |
| **Zeitgeist** | The defining spirit/mood of a particular period| Remote work perfectly captures the *zeitgeist* of the post-2020 era. |
| **Schadenfreude**| Pleasure derived from another's misfortune | He felt a twinge of *schadenfreude* when his rival's code failed to compile. |
| **Doppelgänger**| An apparition or double of a living person | I saw your *doppelgänger* at the tech conference! |
| **Wunderkind** | A person who achieves great success when young | The 19-year-old developer is the new *wunderkind* of Silicon Valley. |

## 🧠 Practice Questions

---
**Q1. The lawyer stated that, _____, the evidence was strong enough to proceed with the lawsuit.**

(A) En masse  
(B) Prima facie  
(C) Faux pas  
(D) Ad hoc  

**✅ Answer: (B)**

**📖 Explanation:** 'Prima facie' means 'at first sight' or 'based on first impression'. The lawyer means that on first look, the evidence is strong.
**❌ Why (A) is wrong:** En masse means in a large group.  
**❌ Why (C) is wrong:** Faux pas means a social blunder.  
**❌ Why (D) is wrong:** Ad hoc means for a specific purpose.

**📏 Rule:** Contextual Foreign Word Application  
**⚡ Shortcut:** Law/Evidence context almost always pairs with Prima Facie, De facto, or Bona fide.  
**📊 Difficulty:** Medium  
**🏢 Company:** TCS NQT
