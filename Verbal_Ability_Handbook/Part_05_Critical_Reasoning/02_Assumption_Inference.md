## 1. What is an Assumption?
An assumption is the **UNSTATED PREMISE** in an argument. It is the hidden bridge that connects the author's facts to their conclusion. 
If the assumption is false, the entire argument collapses.

``` rule
Argument = Premise (Fact) + Assumption (Hidden Fact) -> Conclusion (Opinion)
```

**Example:**
- *Premise:* Raj studied for 10 hours a day.
- *Conclusion:* Raj will top the exam.
- *Assumption:* Studying for 10 hours a day is sufficient to top the exam. (If this is false, he won't top).

---

## 2. The Negation Test (The Ultimate Hack)
How do you prove that an option is the correct assumption? Use the **Negation Test**.

**Steps:**
1. Take the option you think is correct.
2. Negate it (Make it negative by adding "NOT" or reversing the meaning).
3. Read the argument with this negated statement.
4. **Result:** If the argument is DESTROYED, the option was the correct assumption. If the argument still survives, the option was just extra information.

**Let's test it on the previous example:**
*Option A:* Studying 10 hours a day is enough to top.
*Negated Option A:* Studying 10 hours a day is NOT enough to top.
*Impact:* If it's not enough, Raj won't top. The conclusion is destroyed! Therefore, Option A is the correct assumption.

---

## 3. What is an Inference?
An inference is a conclusion that **MUST BE TRUE** based purely on the facts provided in the passage. You cannot use outside knowledge. 

> [!CAUTION]
> **Inference vs. Assumption:**
> An **Assumption** comes *before* the conclusion (it builds the argument).
> An **Inference** comes *after* the facts (it is a logical deduction from the facts).

### The Golden Rules of Inference:
1. **No Outside Info:** If it's not in the text, it cannot be an inference.
2. **Beware of Extreme Language:** Words like *All, Always, Never, None, Only, Every* make an option very hard to prove. 90% of the time, options with these words are wrong.
3. **Look for Soft Language:** Words like *Some, Many, Sometimes, Often, May, Can, Likely* are easier to prove and are frequently correct.

## 4. Practice Questions: Assumptions (50 Concept Drills)

---
**Q1. Statement:** "To increase our profit margins, we must completely stop manufacturing our line of budget smartphones and focus solely on our premium models." — Smartphone CEO.

Which of the following is an assumption made by the CEO?
(A) The premium models are currently the best-selling phones in the market.
(B) The profit margin on premium models is significantly higher than on budget models.
(C) Customers will stop buying budget smartphones next year.
(D) The company cannot manufacture both budget and premium models simultaneously.

**✅ Answer: (B)**
**📖 Explanation:** Let's use the Negation Test on (B). "The profit margin on premium models is NOT significantly higher than on budget models." If this is true, shifting focus to premium models will NOT increase overall profit margins. The CEO's conclusion is destroyed. Therefore, (B) is the assumption.
**❌ Why (A) is wrong:** Sales volume doesn't equal profit margin.
**❌ Why (D) is wrong:** They can manufacture both, but the CEO wants to maximize profit margins by dropping the lower-margin item.
**📏 Rule:** The Negation Test.
**📊 Difficulty:** Medium
**🏢 Company:** TCS

---
**Q2. Statement:** Due to the rising number of accidents on Highway 5, the speed limit is being reduced from 80 km/h to 60 km/h.

What is assumed here?
(A) Driving at 80 km/h causes vehicles to break down.
(B) Most of the accidents on Highway 5 are caused by high speeds.
(C) Drivers will completely stop using Highway 5.
(D) 60 km/h is the safest speed limit in the world.

**✅ Answer: (B)**
**📖 Explanation:** The authorities are lowering the speed limit to stop accidents. They must assume speed is the cause. Negation Test: "Most accidents on Highway 5 are NOT caused by high speeds (e.g., they are caused by bad roads)." If true, lowering the speed limit won't stop the accidents.
**📊 Difficulty:** Easy
**🏢 Company:** Wipro

## 5. Practice Questions: Inferences (50 Concept Drills)

---
**Q3. Passage:** In 2020, Company Alpha generated 60% of its total revenue from software subscriptions, 30% from hardware sales, and 10% from consulting services. In 2021, total revenue doubled, but the revenue from hardware sales remained exactly the same in dollar amount as in 2020.

Which of the following can be inferred?
(A) Revenue from software subscriptions increased in 2021.
(B) Hardware sales represented less than 30% of total revenue in 2021.
(C) Consulting services generated the least amount of revenue in 2021.
(D) Company Alpha stopped focusing on hardware sales.

**✅ Answer: (B)**
**📖 Explanation:** It is a math-based inference. Let total revenue in 2020 = $100. Hardware = $30.
In 2021, total revenue doubles to $200. The passage says hardware revenue stayed the *same in dollar amount* ($30). 
Therefore, hardware in 2021 = $30 out of $200, which is 15%. 15% is less than 30%. This MUST be true.
**❌ Why (A) is wrong:** We don't know the exact breakdown of software vs consulting in 2021.
**❌ Why (D) is wrong:** We can't infer intent (focus), only mathematical facts.
**📏 Rule:** Must Be True based on facts.
**📊 Difficulty:** Hard
**🏢 Company:** Infosys, IBM

---
**Q4. Passage:** Most start-ups in the fintech sector fail within their first three years due to a lack of regulatory compliance. 'PaySwift' is a fintech start-up that was founded four years ago.

What can be logically inferred?
(A) PaySwift strictly followed all regulatory compliance.
(B) PaySwift will never fail.
(C) PaySwift survived beyond the typical failure window of most fintech start-ups.
(D) All fintech startups fail.

**✅ Answer: (C)**
**📖 Explanation:** The passage says "most fail within three years." PaySwift has been around for four years. Therefore, it survived beyond that three-year window. This is a direct extraction of fact.
**❌ Why (A) is wrong:** We don't know WHY they survived. Maybe they had great funding despite poor compliance.
**❌ Why (B) is wrong:** Extreme language ("never").
**❌ Why (D) is wrong:** Extreme language ("All" vs "Most").
**📏 Rule:** Avoid Extreme Language.
**📊 Difficulty:** Easy
**🏢 Company:** Cognizant

## Key Takeaways
- **Assumptions** must be verified using the **Negation Test**.
- **Inferences** must be 100% provable using ONLY the provided text.
- Beware of extreme words in Inference options (*All, Every, Never*). They are almost always traps.
