## Mock 04: Cognizant GenC Pattern

**Exam Details:**
- **Time Allowed:** 25 Minutes
- **Total Questions:** 25
- **Focus Areas:** Para jumbles (High difficulty), Reading Comprehension, Contextual Vocabulary.

### Questions (Representative Hard Set)

**Q1. Arrange the sentences to form a coherent paragraph:**
A) This creates a continuous cycle of learning and unlearning.
B) In the rapidly evolving tech landscape, skills become obsolete very quickly.
C) Therefore, adaptability is considered the most crucial trait for modern software engineers.
D) What was cutting-edge five years ago is now considered legacy technology.

(A) BDAC
(B) BADC
(C) DBAC
(D) DBCA

**✅ Answer: (A)**
**📖 Explanation:** B introduces the main topic (skills becoming obsolete). D provides evidence for B (what was cutting edge is now legacy). A explains the consequence of this rapid change (a cycle of learning). C provides the logical conclusion (Therefore, adaptability is crucial). 
**📊 Difficulty:** Hard

**Q2. Read the passage and answer:**
*Passage:* The proliferation of IoT devices has undoubtedly improved convenience, from smart thermostats to intelligent refrigerators. However, the security infrastructure of these devices remains nascent. Unlike traditional computing systems, many IoT devices lack the processing power to run complex encryption algorithms, rendering them vulnerable to botnet recruitment.
*Question:* What can be inferred about the manufacturer's priority regarding IoT devices based on the passage?
(A) Manufacturers prioritize security over convenience.
(B) Manufacturers prioritize convenience and functionality over robust security.
(C) Manufacturers are completely unaware of botnet threats.
(D) Manufacturers design devices explicitly for botnets.

**✅ Answer: (B)**
**📖 Explanation:** The passage states IoT has improved convenience but security remains "nascent" (underdeveloped) and devices "lack processing power for encryption". This implies convenience was prioritized over security.

## Mock 05: Wipro NLTH Pattern

**Exam Details:**

**Q1. Change the voice:**
The mechanic has repaired my car.
(A) My car has been repaired by the mechanic.
(B) My car is being repaired by the mechanic.
(C) My car was repaired by the mechanic.
(D) My car had been repaired by the mechanic.

**✅ Answer: (A)**
**📖 Explanation:** Present Perfect (has repaired) becomes `has been + V3` (has been repaired).

## Mocks 07 to 11: Company Specific Overviews

**Mock 07: IBM Pattern (20q, 20m)**
IBM focuses heavily on **Sentence Correction**. You will be given a long sentence with a bolded phrase and must choose the best replacement among the options. Testing idiom usage and parallelism.

**Mock 08: HCL Pattern (30q, 30m)**
HCL tests basic grammar but with a higher volume of questions. Emphasis on prepositions and articles.

**Mock 09: EY Pattern (25q, 25m)**
As a consulting firm, EY tests **Critical Reasoning** heavily. Expect "Statement and Conclusion", "Strong and Weak Arguments", and "Course of Action" questions mixed into the verbal section.

**Mock 10: Deloitte Pattern (20q, 20m)**
Deloitte emphasizes high-level Reading Comprehension. Passages are often related to economics, corporate ethics, or emerging tech.

**Mock 11: ZS Associates Pattern (30q, 30m) - Expert Level**
ZS is known for one of the hardest verbal sections, focusing on Data Interpretation through text (logical puzzles presented in paragraph form) and heavy vocabulary.
