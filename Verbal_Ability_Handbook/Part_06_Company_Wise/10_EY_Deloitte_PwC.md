### 10.1 Comparative Pattern Analysis

#### EY (Ernst & Young)
- **Focus:** EY often uses a specialized business English test (like Versant or a tailored AMCAT).
- **Nuance:** High focus on tone. You must know the difference between formal, semi-formal, and informal language. Email writing or error spotting in business correspondence is common.

#### Deloitte
- **Focus:** Deloitte's test is heavily logical. 
- **Nuance:** You will see complex Para Jumbles, Critical Reasoning, and Reading Comprehension passages based on economics, auditing, and global markets.

#### PwC
- **Focus:** Core grammar and vocabulary.
- **Nuance:** Tests your fundamental grasp of English syntax. High emphasis on prepositions, articles, and tense consistency.

``` rule
THE BIG 4 CORPORATE TONE RULE:
Whenever answering a question about vocabulary or sentence improvement for the Big 4, select the option that is most Polite, Direct, and Formal. 
Avoid slang, phrasal verbs that are casual (e.g., use "investigate" instead of "look into"), and passive-aggressive tones.
```

**Q1. (EY) Which of the following sentences is the most appropriate and formal way to request information from a client?**

(A) Send me the files I asked for yesterday.  
(B) I would appreciate it if you could send the requested files at your earliest convenience.  
(C) Need those files ASAP, please.  
(D) Kindly revert back with the files.  

**✅ Answer: (B)**

**📖 Explanation:** Option B is polite, formal, and professional. Option A is too direct/rude. Option C uses unprofessional abbreviations (ASAP). Option D uses "revert back", which is grammatically redundant (revert means to return to a previous state, not to reply).

**📏 Rule:** Corporate Tone / Email Etiquette  
**⚡ Shortcut:** "I would appreciate it if..." is the gold standard for formal requests.  
**📊 Difficulty:** Medium  
**🏢 Company:** EY, PwC  
---

**Q2. (Deloitte) Critical Reasoning:**
Statement: The company has decided to offer a 4-day work week to increase employee productivity, assuming that a longer weekend will reduce burnout.
Which of the following, if true, most WEAKENS the company's assumption?

(A) Employees will have to work 10 hours a day instead of 8 to complete their 40 hours.  
(B) Studies show that working more than 8 hours a day significantly increases daily exhaustion and burnout.  
(C) Competitor companies still work 5 days a week.  
(D) Employees are excited about the 3-day weekend.  

**✅ Answer: (B)**

**📖 Explanation:** The company assumes the 4-day week will *reduce* burnout. Option B states that compressing the hours into 4 days (which implies longer daily hours) actually *increases* daily exhaustion and burnout. This directly attacks the core assumption.

**📏 Rule:** Critical Reasoning (Weakening Arguments)  
**⚡ Shortcut:** Find the core assumption (3-day weekend = less burnout). Find the option that proves it wrong (10-hour days = more burnout).  
**📊 Difficulty:** Hard  
**🏢 Company:** Deloitte  
---

**Q3. (PwC) Identify the error in the sentence:**
(A) The manager, along with / (B) the auditing team, / (C) have decided to / (D) review the accounts again.

(A) A  
(B) B  
(C) C  
(D) D  

**✅ Answer: (C)**

**📖 Explanation:** "Along with" does not compound the subject. The main subject is "The manager" (singular). Therefore, the verb must be singular ("has decided", not "have decided").

**📏 Rule:** Subject-Verb Agreement (Accompaniment)  
**⚡ Shortcut:** Ignore everything set off by commas ("along with..."). Manager + has.  
**📊 Difficulty:** Medium  
**🏢 Company:** PwC, EY  
---

**Q4. (EY) Fill in the blank with the most appropriate corporate vocabulary:**
To ensure compliance with the new tax laws, the finance department will conduct a thorough ______ of all recent transactions.

(A) inspection  
(B) audit  
(C) look  
(D) glance  

**✅ Answer: (B)**

**📖 Explanation:** In finance and accounting (Big 4 domain), a thorough examination of financial records for compliance is called an "audit". "Inspection" is for physical objects or processes.

**📏 Rule:** Business Vocabulary  
**⚡ Shortcut:** Financial review = Audit.  
**📊 Difficulty:** Easy  
**🏢 Company:** EY, Deloitte  
---

**Q5. (Deloitte) Rearrange to form a paragraph:**
A. This rapid inflation forced the central bank to intervene.
B. The government injected billions into the economy during the crisis.
C. Consequently, they raised interest rates by 50 basis points.
D. As a result of the surplus money supply, consumer prices surged.

(A) BDAC  
(B) BACD  
(C) ABCD  
(D) DBAC  

**✅ Answer: (A)**

**📖 Explanation:** 
1. B is the root cause (injecting billions).
2. D is the immediate effect of B (surplus money -> prices surged).
3. A refers to the "surged prices" as "This rapid inflation" and introduces the bank's action.
4. C concludes with the specific action taken ("Consequently, they raised...").

**📏 Rule:** Para Jumbles (Cause and Effect / Economic Logic)  
**⚡ Shortcut:** "Surged prices" in D = "rapid inflation" in A. D->A is a mandatory pair. Only option A has DA.  
**📊 Difficulty:** Hard  
**🏢 Company:** Deloitte  
---

**Q6. (PwC) Choose the correct preposition:**
The client was not amenable ______ the proposed changes in the contract.

(A) for  
(B) to  
(C) with  
(D) on  

**✅ Answer: (B)**

**📖 Explanation:** The adjective "amenable" (meaning open and responsive to suggestion; easily persuaded or controlled) takes the preposition "to".

**📏 Rule:** Fixed Prepositions  
**⚡ Shortcut:** Amenable TO (suggestions/changes).  
**📊 Difficulty:** Hard  
**🏢 Company:** PwC  
---

**Q7. (EY) Sentence Improvement:**
The HR department asked the candidate *to provide a brief summary of* his previous experience.

(A) to provide a briefly summary of  
(B) to briefly summarize  
(C) to provide a brief summarize of  
(D) No improvement  

**✅ Answer: (B)**

**📖 Explanation:** "Provide a brief summary of" is grammatically correct, but "briefly summarize" is more concise and removes the redundancy (a summary is inherently brief). In strict business English, conciseness wins. However, if (B) was not an option, (D) would be acceptable. Between the two, (B) is tighter.
*Note: In many standard tests, (D) is accepted, but Big 4 tests heavily penalize wordiness.*

**📏 Rule:** Conciseness / Removing Redundancy  
**⚡ Shortcut:** Summary = brief. "Brief summary" is redundant. Just "summarize".  
**📊 Difficulty:** Medium  
**🏢 Company:** EY  
---

**Q8. (Deloitte) Vocabulary:**
Choose the synonym for: **AMBIGUOUS**

(A) Explicit  
(B) Equivocal  
(C) Lucid  
(D) Definitive  

**✅ Answer: (B)**

**📖 Explanation:** Ambiguous means open to more than one interpretation; unclear. Equivocal means the same. Explicit, Lucid, and Definitive all mean clear and clear-cut (antonyms).

**📏 Rule:** Advanced Vocabulary  
**⚡ Shortcut:** Ambi = Both. Equi = Equal. Both imply two voices/meanings, hence unclear.  
**📊 Difficulty:** Hard  
**🏢 Company:** Deloitte  
---

**Q9. (PwC) Identify the error:**
The CEO, as well as the board members, (A) / are responsible for (B) / the strategic direction (C) / of the company. (D)

(A) A  
(B) B  
(C) C  
(D) D  

**✅ Answer: (B)**

**📖 Explanation:** Similar to "along with", the phrase "as well as" does not create a plural subject. The verb must agree with the first subject, "The CEO" (singular). "Are" should be "is".

**📏 Rule:** SVA (Accompaniment)  
**⚡ Shortcut:** The CEO... is responsible.  
**📊 Difficulty:** Medium  
**🏢 Company:** PwC

**Passage:**
The transition from Libor (London Interbank Offered Rate) to alternative reference rates (ARRs) represents a seismic shift in global financial markets. For decades, Libor underpinned trillions of dollars in financial contracts, from corporate loans to complex derivatives. However, following manipulation scandals, regulatory bodies mandated its phase-out. The primary challenge in this transition lies in "fallback language"—the legal provisions in existing contracts that dictate what happens when Libor is no longer available. Because ARRs are structurally different from Libor (e.g., they are backward-looking overnight rates rather than forward-looking term rates), simply swapping one rate for another without adjusting the spread can result in significant value transfer between contractual parties.

**Q10. According to the passage, why is Libor being phased out?**

(A) It was too complicated to calculate.  
(B) It caused the 2008 financial crisis.  
(C) Due to manipulation scandals.  
(D) Because ARRs are forward-looking.  

**✅ Answer: (C)**

**📖 Explanation:** The passage states: "However, following manipulation scandals, regulatory bodies mandated its phase-out."

**📏 Rule:** RC - Fact Retrieval  
**⚡ Shortcut:** Scan for "phase-out" and look at the reason immediately preceding it.  
**📊 Difficulty:** Easy  
**🏢 Company:** Deloitte

**Q13. (EY) Fill in the blanks:**
The consultant was known for her ______ approach; she never relied on theory, but always on ______ evidence.

**Q15. (Deloitte) Critical Reasoning:**
Statement: Most startups fail within the first five years due to a lack of market need for their product, rather than a lack of funding.
Conclusion: Therefore, extensive market research is more crucial for startup survival than securing venture capital.
Which of the following strengthens the conclusion?

(A) Venture capitalists rarely fund startups without market research.  
(B) Startups with immense funding but no market research have a 90% failure rate.  
(C) Market research is very expensive.  
(D) Some startups succeed without any market research.  

**✅ Answer: (B)**

**📖 Explanation:** Option B strengthens the conclusion by showing that even WITH funding, a lack of market research leads to failure, proving that market research is indeed the more crucial factor.

**📏 Rule:** Critical Reasoning (Strengthening Arguments)  
**⚡ Shortcut:** Prove that Factor A (Market Research) > Factor B (Funding). Option B literally does this.  
**📊 Difficulty:** Medium  
**🏢 Company:** Deloitte

**Q16. (EY) Sentence Correction:**
The financial projections for the next quarter *looks highly promising*.

(A) look highly promising  
(B) looks high promising  
(C) look highly promised  
(D) No correction required  

**✅ Answer: (A)**

**📖 Explanation:** The subject is "projections" (plural). Therefore, the verb must be plural "look" (without 's').

**📏 Rule:** Subject-Verb Agreement  
**⚡ Shortcut:** Projections (plural) -> look.  
**📊 Difficulty:** Easy

**Q17. (PwC) Fill in the blank with the correct tense:**
By the time the auditors arrive tomorrow, the finance team ______ all the reports.

(A) will finish  
(B) will have finished  
(C) have finished  
(D) finished  

**✅ Answer: (B)**

**📖 Explanation:** An action that will be completed before a certain point of time in the future requires the Future Perfect tense ("will have + V3").

**📏 Rule:** Future Perfect Tense  
**⚡ Shortcut:** "By the time [future]" -> Will have + V3.  
**📊 Difficulty:** Medium  
**🏢 Company:** PwC  
---

**Q18. (Deloitte) Analogy:**
**REVENUE : EXPENSES :: PROFIT : ?**

(A) Loss  
(B) Deficit  
(C) Margin  
(D) Debt  

**✅ Answer: (A)**

**📖 Explanation:** Revenue is the opposite of Expenses in a cash flow sense. Profit is the opposite of Loss on an income statement. (Deficit is usually the opposite of Surplus).

**📏 Rule:** Analogies (Finance Context)  
**⚡ Shortcut:** Find the exact financial antonym.  
**📊 Difficulty:** Easy  
**🏢 Company:** Deloitte  
---

**Q19. (EY) Which word is redundant in the following sentence?**
The two companies decided to merge together to form a larger conglomerate.

(A) companies  
(B) merge  
(C) together  
(D) larger  

**✅ Answer: (C)**

**📖 Explanation:** "Merge" means to combine or come together. Therefore, saying "merge together" is a tautology (redundant).

**📏 Rule:** Redundancy / Tautology  
**⚡ Shortcut:** Merge = combine together. Drop "together".  
**📊 Difficulty:** Medium  
**🏢 Company:** EY  
---

**Q20. (PwC) Active to Passive:**
The management must address the employees' grievances.

(A) The employees' grievances must be addressed by the management.  
(B) The employees' grievances have to be addressed by the management.  
(C) The employees' grievances must have been addressed by the management.  
(D) The employees' grievances should be addressed by the management.  

**✅ Answer: (A)**

**📖 Explanation:** The sentence uses a modal verb ("must"). The passive structure is Modal + be + V3 ("must be addressed").

**📏 Rule:** Passive Voice with Modals  
**⚡ Shortcut:** Modal -> Modal + be + V3. Don't change "must" to "should".  
**📊 Difficulty:** Easy  
**🏢 Company:** PwC

### 10.4 Big 4 Quick Reference & Study Plan

#### Top 10 Topics to Study for the Big 4
1. Business Email Etiquette (Tone, Formality, Redundancy)
2. Critical Reasoning (Strengthen/Weaken arguments)
3. Reading Comprehension (Economics, Audit, Tech passages)
4. Advanced Corporate Vocabulary (Pragmatic, Empirical, Mitigate, Audit)
5. Redundancy / Wordiness (Revert back, Merge together)
6. Subject-Verb Agreement (Along with, As well as)
7. Future Perfect Tense (By the time...)
8. Prepositions with adjectives (Amenable to, Consistent with)
9. Logical Para Jumbles (Cause & Effect)
10. Spelling of professional words (Liaison, Questionnaire, Occurrence)

#### 3-Week Big 4 Study Plan

| **Week** | **Focus Area** | **Daily Action Items** |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Week 1** | Grammar & Tone | Focus heavily on SVA and removing redundancy. Read 5 sample professional emails daily. Notice the structure (polite opening, concise body, formal closing). |
| **Week 2** | Logic & RC | Practice 10 Critical Reasoning questions daily. Read financial news (Wall Street Journal, The Economist) to get comfortable with the vocabulary used in Deloitte/EY RCs. |
| **Week 3** | Vocab & Speed | Memorize Big 4 specific vocabulary (e.g., amortization, paradigm, holistic, synergy). Take timed mocks to build speed. |

> [!WARNING]  
> Do not use colloquial English or slang rules. The Big 4 tests are looking for polished, corporate-ready communication skills. If an option sounds like something you'd text a friend, it is the wrong answer.
