### 13.2 Comprehensive Practice Questions

Here are 15 highly representative questions for Hexaware and DXC.

**Q1. (Hexaware) Choose the correctly spelt word:**

(A) Embarrassment  
(B) Embarassment  
(C) Embarasment  
(D) Emmbarrassment  

**✅ Answer: (A)**

**📖 Explanation:** The word contains a double 'r' and a double 's'.

**📏 Rule:** Spelling Rules  
**⚡ Shortcut:** Remember: Two R's and Two S's in embaRR aSS ment.  
**📊 Difficulty:** Medium  
**🏢 Company:** Hexaware  
---

**Q2. (DXC) Fill in the blank:**
I have been waiting for the bus ______ two hours.

(A) since  
(B) for  
(C) from  
(D) about  

**✅ Answer: (B)**

**📖 Explanation:** "Two hours" is a duration/period of time. Therefore, we use "for".

**📏 Rule:** Prepositions (Since vs For)  
**⚡ Shortcut:** Number + Time Unit (two hours, five days) = FOR.  
**📊 Difficulty:** Easy  
**🏢 Company:** DXC, Hexaware  
---

**Q3. (Hexaware) Identify the error:**
One of the most (A) / beautiful city (B) / in the world (C) / is Paris. (D)

(A) A  
(B) B  
(C) C  
(D) D  

**✅ Answer: (B)**

**📖 Explanation:** The phrase "One of the" must always be followed by a plural noun. It should be "beautiful cities".

**📏 Rule:** Subject-Verb Agreement / Noun rules  
**⚡ Shortcut:** One out of MANY. So the noun must be plural.  
**📊 Difficulty:** Medium  
**🏢 Company:** Hexaware  
---

**Q4. (DXC) Active to Passive Voice:**
The manager will send the email tomorrow.

(A) The email will be send by the manager tomorrow.  
(B) The email would be sent by the manager tomorrow.  
(C) The email will be sent by the manager tomorrow.  
(D) The email will have been sent by the manager tomorrow.  

**✅ Answer: (C)**

**📖 Explanation:** Future Simple tense ("will send"). Passive structure: will be + V3 ("will be sent"). Option A uses V1 ("send") which is wrong.

**📏 Rule:** Voice (Future Simple)  
**⚡ Shortcut:** Will + V1 -> Will be + V3.  
**📊 Difficulty:** Easy  
**🏢 Company:** DXC  
---

**Q5. (Hexaware) Choose the synonym for: ADEQUATE**

(A) Insufficient  
(B) Abundant  
(C) Sufficient  
(D) Lacking  

**✅ Answer: (C)**

**📖 Explanation:** Adequate means satisfactory or acceptable in quality or quantity; sufficient.

**📏 Rule:** Basic Vocabulary  
**⚡ Shortcut:** Adequate = Enough = Sufficient.  
**📊 Difficulty:** Easy  
**🏢 Company:** Hexaware  
---

**Q6. (DXC) Sentence Improvement:**
I look forward *to meet* you next week.

(A) to meeting  
(B) to have met  
(C) meeting  
(D) No improvement  

**✅ Answer: (A)**

**📖 Explanation:** The phrase "look forward to" is a prepositional phrase where "to" is a preposition, not part of an infinitive verb. Prepositions must be followed by a noun or a gerund (verb+ing). Therefore, it must be "to meeting".

**📏 Rule:** Gerunds after Prepositions  
**⚡ Shortcut:** "Look forward to" ALWAYS takes an -ing verb.  
**📊 Difficulty:** Hard  
**🏢 Company:** DXC, TCS  
---

**Q7. (Hexaware) Fill in the blank with the appropriate article:**
He is ______ honest man.

(A) a  
(B) an  
(C) the  
(D) No article  

**✅ Answer: (B)**

**📖 Explanation:** The word "honest" begins with a silent 'h'. The phonetic sound is a vowel (o-nest). Therefore, it takes "an".

**📏 Rule:** Articles (Phonetics)  
**⚡ Shortcut:** Sound it out. Vowel sound = An.  
**📊 Difficulty:** Easy  
**🏢 Company:** Hexaware  
---

**Q8. (DXC) Rearrange the sentences:**
P. However, the initial results were disappointing.
Q. The marketing team launched a new campaign last month.
R. They hope this will improve customer engagement.
S. To fix this, they tweaked the social media strategy.

(A) QPSR  
(B) QSPR  
(C) PQRS  
(D) RQPS  

**✅ Answer: (A)**

**📖 Explanation:** 
1. Q introduces the campaign.
2. P provides the immediate negative result ("However... disappointing").
3. S offers the solution to P ("To fix this...").
4. R concludes with the goal of the solution ("hope this will improve...").

**📏 Rule:** Para Jumbles (Problem-Solution)  
**⚡ Shortcut:** Q is the opener. P gives a problem. S gives a fix. PS is a mandatory pair. QPSR fits perfectly.  
**📊 Difficulty:** Medium  
**🏢 Company:** DXC  
---

**Q9. (Hexaware) What does the idiom "A piece of cake" mean?**

(A) A tasty dessert.  
(B) Something very easy to do.  
(C) A small share of the profit.  
(D) A difficult task.  

**✅ Answer: (B)**

**📖 Explanation:** "A piece of cake" means a task that can be accomplished very easily.

**📏 Rule:** Basic Idioms  
**⚡ Shortcut:** Piece of cake = Easy.  
**📊 Difficulty:** Easy  
**🏢 Company:** Hexaware

**Q10. (DXC) Direct to Indirect Speech:**
She said, "I am reading a book."

(A) She said that she is reading a book.  
(B) She said that she was reading a book.  
(C) She told that she was reading a book.  
(D) She said that I was reading a book.  

**✅ Answer: (B)**

**📖 Explanation:** Present Continuous ("am reading") changes to Past Continuous ("was reading"). "I" changes to "she".

**📏 Rule:** Indirect Speech (Tense Change)  
**⚡ Shortcut:** Present Continuous -> Past Continuous.  
**📊 Difficulty:** Easy  
**🏢 Company:** DXC  
---

**Q11. (Hexaware) Antonym for: TRANSPARENT**

(A) Clear  
(B) Opaque  
(C) Translucent  
(D) Fragile  

**✅ Answer: (B)**

**📖 Explanation:** Transparent means allowing light to pass through so that objects behind can be distinctly seen. Opaque means not able to be seen through; not transparent.

**📏 Rule:** Vocabulary  
**⚡ Shortcut:** Transparent = See through. Opaque = Blocked.  
**📊 Difficulty:** Easy  
**🏢 Company:** Hexaware  
---

**Q12. (DXC) Identify the error:**
Neither of the two brothers (A) / are eligible (B) / to participate (C) / in the competition. (D)

(A) A  
(B) B  
(C) C  
(D) D  

**✅ Answer: (B)**

**📖 Explanation:** "Neither of" takes a plural noun ("brothers") but a SINGULAR verb. "Are" should be "is".

**📏 Rule:** SVA (Distributive Pronouns)  
**⚡ Shortcut:** Neither of = Singular verb.  
**📊 Difficulty:** Medium  
**🏢 Company:** DXC  
---

**Q13. (Hexaware) Fill in the blanks (Prepositions):**
The book was placed ______ the table ______ the lamp.

(A) on, besides  
(B) upon, beside  
(C) on, beside  
(D) above, next  

**✅ Answer: (C)**

**📖 Explanation:** "On" means resting upon a surface. "Beside" means next to. ("Besides" with an 's' means 'in addition to', which is incorrect here).

**📏 Rule:** Prepositions (Beside vs Besides)  
**⚡ Shortcut:** Beside = Next to. Besides = In addition to.  
**📊 Difficulty:** Medium  
**🏢 Company:** Hexaware  
---

**Q14. (DXC) Sentence Completion:**
If you had asked me, I ______ you.

(A) will help  
(B) would help  
(C) would have helped  
(D) helped  

**✅ Answer: (C)**

**📖 Explanation:** Third Conditional. If + Past Perfect (had asked) -> Subject + would have + V3.

**📏 Rule:** Third Conditional  
**⚡ Shortcut:** Had + V3 -> Would have + V3.  
**📊 Difficulty:** Medium  
**🏢 Company:** DXC  
---

The CEO’s decision was **arbitrary**, with no clear logical basis.

(A) Well-planned  
(B) Random

(D) Calculated  

**✅ Answer: (B)**

**📖 Explanation:** Arbitrary means based on random choice or personal whim, rather than any reason or system. 

**📏 Rule:** Contextual Vocabulary  
**⚡ Shortcut:** "no clear logical basis" = Random.  
**📊 Difficulty:** Easy  
**🏢 Company:** Hexaware
