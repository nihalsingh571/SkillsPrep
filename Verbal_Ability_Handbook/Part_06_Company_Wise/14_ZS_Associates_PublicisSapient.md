**Q1. (ZS Associates) Critical Reasoning:**
Statement: Over the last decade, sales of organic food have skyrocketed. Researchers conclude that consumers are becoming more health-conscious and are willing to pay a premium for healthier options.
Which of the following, if true, most weakens the researchers' conclusion?

(A) Organic food is proven to have higher nutritional value than non-organic food.  
(B) The price of non-organic food has increased drastically, making the price gap between organic and non-organic negligible.  
(C) Many consumers still prefer the taste of non-organic food.  
(D) Health consciousness is a trend that is popular in urban areas.  

**✅ Answer: (B)**

**📖 Explanation:** The conclusion is that consumers buy organic because they are health-conscious and willing to pay a *premium*. If option B is true, they aren't paying a premium; they are buying it because it now costs the same as non-organic food. This provides an alternative economic reason for the sales increase, weakening the "health-conscious/premium" argument.

**📏 Rule:** CR - Weakening an Argument (Alternative Cause)  
**⚡ Shortcut:** Find the alternate reason. If price gap is gone, people buy it for economics, not health.  
**📊 Difficulty:** Expert  
**🏢 Company:** ZS Associates

**Q2. (Publicis Sapient) Fill in the blanks:**
The CEO’s ______ speech was intended to ______ the fears of the investors, but it instead ______ the panic.

(A) eloquent, aggravate, reduced  
(B) ambiguous, allay, exacerbated  
(C) reassuring, instigate, pacified  
(D) lengthy, augment, minimized  

**✅ Answer: (B)**

**📖 Explanation:** The sentence structure sets up a contrast with "but it instead". The intent was positive (reduce/allay fears). The result was negative (increased/exacerbated panic). An "ambiguous" (unclear) speech would logically cause this negative outcome.

**📏 Rule:** Triple Fillers / Tone Mapping  
**⚡ Shortcut:** Intent = Positive (+). Result = Negative (-). "Allay" (+) means to soothe. "Exacerbated" (-) means made worse.  
**📊 Difficulty:** Hard  
**🏢 Company:** Publicis Sapient

**Q3. (ZS Associates) Identify the error:**
The dataset, comprising of (A) / millions of individual consumer records, (B) / was analyzed to (C) / find purchasing trends. (D)

(A) A  
(B) B  
(C) C  
(D) D  

**✅ Answer: (A)**

**📖 Explanation:** The verb "comprise" means "to consist of". Therefore, saying "comprising of" is redundant. It should be either "comprising millions..." or "composed of millions...".

**📏 Rule:** Redundant Prepositions  
**⚡ Shortcut:** Comprise = Consist of. Never use "comprise of".  
**📊 Difficulty:** Hard  
**🏢 Company:** ZS Associates, Publicis Sapient

**Passage:**
The pharmaceutical industry's reliance on patents to recoup R&D costs has sparked a complex ethical debate. Proponents argue that without the temporary monopoly granted by patents, there would be no financial incentive to invest billions in drug discovery. Conversely, critics contend that this system artificially inflates prices, restricting access to life-saving medications in developing nations. **Q4. What is the primary function of the second paragraph (beginning with "Conversely") in relation to the first?**

(A) To provide an example supporting the first paragraph.  
(B) To introduce a counter-argument to the premise of the first paragraph.  
(C) To offer a solution to the problem stated in the first paragraph.  
(D) To redefine a term used in the first paragraph.  

**✅ Answer: (B)**

**📖 Explanation:** The first sentence argues for patents (incentive). The sentence starting with "Conversely" provides the counter-argument (inflates prices, restricts access). 

**📏 Rule:** RC - Structural Analysis  
**⚡ Shortcut:** "Conversely" or "However" always introduces a counter-argument or contrast.  
**📊 Difficulty:** Medium  
**🏢 Company:** ZS Associates

**Q7. (Publicis Sapient) Sentence Improvement:**
Having worked late into the night, *the report was finally finished by her*.

(A) she finally finished the report.  
(B) finally the report was finished.  
(C) it was finally finished by her.  
(D) No improvement.  

**✅ Answer: (A)**

**📖 Explanation:** Dangling modifier. The phrase "Having worked late into the night" must logically modify the subject that follows the comma. The "report" did not work late into the night; "she" did. Therefore, the subject of the main clause must be "she".

**📏 Rule:** Dangling Modifiers  
**⚡ Shortcut:** Who worked late? She did. "She" must immediately follow the comma.  
**📊 Difficulty:** Hard  
**🏢 Company:** Publicis Sapient, ZS Associates

**Q8. (ZS Associates) Critical Reasoning:**
Statement: A software company replaced its open-plan office with individual cubicles to reduce noise. Six months later, productivity dropped by 15%.
Conclusion: Individual cubicles decrease productivity.
Which of the following is an assumption required by the conclusion?

(A) Open-plan offices are cheaper to maintain than cubicles.  
(B) The reduction in noise did not cause the drop in productivity.  
(C) No other major changes (like management shifts or budget cuts) occurred during those six months that could have caused the drop in productivity.  
(D) Employees prefer open-plan offices.  

**✅ Answer: (C)**

**📖 Explanation:** To conclude that A (cubicles) caused B (lower productivity), one must assume that C (some other lurking variable) did not cause B. If there were budget cuts, that might have caused the drop. The argument *assumes* all other variables remained constant.

**📏 Rule:** CR - Assumptions (Isolating variables)  
**⚡ Shortcut:** Causal arguments always assume there is no alternate cause.  
**📊 Difficulty:** Expert  
**🏢 Company:** ZS Associates

**Q10. (ZS Associates) Vocabulary (Analogy):**
**EPHEMERAL : PERMANENT :: NEOPHYTE : ?**

(A) Beginner  
(B) Expert  
(C) Plant  
(D) Novice  

**✅ Answer: (B)**

**📖 Explanation:** Ephemeral (short-lived) is the antonym of Permanent. Therefore, we need the antonym of Neophyte (a beginner). The antonym is Expert or Veteran.

**📏 Rule:** Word Analogies (Antonyms)  
**⚡ Shortcut:** Neophyte = Neo (new) = Beginner. Opposite = Expert.

**Q11. (Publicis Sapient) Identify the error:**
(A) Scarcely had the API / (B) been integrated than / (C) the entire system / (D) crashed under the load.

(A) A  
(B) B  
(C) C  
(D) D  

**✅ Answer: (B)**

**📖 Explanation:** The correlative conjunction "Scarcely" pairs with "when", not "than". ("No sooner" pairs with "than").

**📏 Rule:** Correlative Conjunctions  
**⚡ Shortcut:** Scarcely/Hardly = WHEN.  
**📊 Difficulty:** Medium

**Q12. (ZS Associates) Rearrange the sentences:**
P. This paradigm shift requires a re-evaluation of data privacy laws.
Q. Machine learning algorithms now process personal data at unprecedented scales.
R. Consequently, traditional legal frameworks are becoming obsolete.
S. Without updated laws, consumer privacy is severely compromised.

(A) QPRS  
(B) PQRS  
(C) QSPR  
(D) RQPS  

**✅ Answer: (A)**

**📖 Explanation:** 
1. Q introduces the premise (Algorithms processing data).
2. P refers to this development as "This paradigm shift".
3. R gives the consequence (old laws are obsolete).
4. S provides the final warning (without updated laws, privacy is compromised).

**📏 Rule:** Para Jumbles (Demonstrative Linking)  
**⚡ Shortcut:** "This paradigm shift" in P refers to the action in Q. QP is a mandatory pair. Only option A starts with QP.  
**📊 Difficulty:** Medium  
**🏢 Company:** ZS Associates

**Q13. (Publicis Sapient) Sentence Correction:**
The committee *are divided* in their opinions on the merger.

(A) is divided  
(B) are dividing  
(C) is dividing  
(D) No correction required  

**✅ Answer: (D)**

**📖 Explanation:** "Committee" is a collective noun. When the group acts as a single unit, it takes a singular verb. However, when the members act individually or disagree (as indicated by "divided in their opinions"), the collective noun takes a plural verb ("are divided").

**📏 Rule:** Collective Nouns (Divided vs Unified)  
**⚡ Shortcut:** Unified group = Singular verb. Divided group = Plural verb.  
**📊 Difficulty:** Expert  
**🏢 Company:** Publicis Sapient, ZS Associates

**Q17. (Publicis Sapient) Active to Passive Voice:**
Someone has altered the database schema.

(A) The database schema was altered.  
(B) The database schema is being altered.  
(C) The database schema has been altered.  
(D) The database schema had been altered.  

**✅ Answer: (C)**

**📖 Explanation:** "Has altered" (Present Perfect) becomes "has been altered". Note that "by someone" is usually dropped in passive voice when the subject is unknown or unimportant.

**📏 Rule:** Voice (Present Perfect / Unknown subject)  
**⚡ Shortcut:** Has + V3 -> Has been + V3.  
**📊 Difficulty:** Easy  
**🏢 Company:** Publicis Sapient

**Q18. (ZS Associates) Critical Reasoning:**
Statement: In order to reduce employee turnover, the company should increase salaries by 20%.
Assumption 1: Salary is the primary reason employees leave the company.
Assumption 2: The company has the financial resources to increase salaries by 20%.
Which assumptions are implicit in the statement?

(A) Only Assumption 1  
(B) Only Assumption 2  
(C) Both 1 and 2  
(D) Neither 1 nor 2  

**✅ Answer: (C)**

**📖 Explanation:** For the proposed solution (raise salaries) to solve the problem (turnover), it assumes that salary is indeed the root cause of the turnover (Assumption 1). Furthermore, for the company to *implement* this proposal, it assumes the company can afford to do so (Assumption 2).

**📏 Rule:** Implicit Assumptions  
**⚡ Shortcut:** A proposed solution assumes the solution addresses the root cause AND that the solution is physically/financially executable.  
**📊 Difficulty:** Hard  
**🏢 Company:** ZS Associates
