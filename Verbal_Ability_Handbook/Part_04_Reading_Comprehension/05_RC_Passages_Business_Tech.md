## Passage 1: The Impact of Artificial Intelligence
Artificial Intelligence (AI) is no longer a futuristic concept confined to sci-fi literature; it is a pervasive reality restructuring the global economy. While historically, technological revolutions—such as the advent of the steam engine—displaced manual labor only to create a surge in manufacturing jobs, AI presents a unique paradigm. It threatens not only routine physical tasks but also cognitive functions traditionally reserved for white-collar professionals. 

Algorithms can now draft legal contracts, diagnose medical anomalies, and write functional code with unprecedented speed. Consequently, the anxiety surrounding job displacement is palpable. However, some economists argue that AI will act as a collaborative tool rather than a replacement. By automating mundane tasks, AI allows humans to focus on higher-order problem-solving, emotional intelligence, and creative endeavors—areas where machines still falter. Ultimately, the transition's success hinges on massive governmental and corporate investments in upskilling the current workforce.

**Q1. What is the primary purpose of the passage?**
(A) To argue that AI will lead to massive unemployment.
(B) To compare AI with the Industrial Revolution.
(C) To discuss the economic impact of AI and the shift in workforce requirements.
(D) To prove that machines cannot possess emotional intelligence.
**✅ Answer: (C)**
**📖 Explanation:** The passage discusses both the threat of job displacement and the opportunity for collaboration, concluding with the need for upskilling. (C) captures this comprehensively.

**Q2. According to the passage, how is AI different from previous technological revolutions?**
(A) It creates manufacturing jobs.
(B) It displaces both manual and cognitive labor.
(C) It is entirely a futuristic concept.
(D) It requires no human intervention.
**✅ Answer: (B)**
**📖 Explanation:** The passage states: "It threatens not only routine physical tasks but also cognitive functions traditionally reserved for white-collar professionals."

**Q3. What does the author imply by the phrase "unique paradigm"?**
(A) A completely unsolvable problem.
(B) A distinct framework or model unlike the past.
(C) A temporary technological glitch.
(D) A legal framework for technology.
**✅ Answer: (B)**
**📖 Explanation:** "Unique paradigm" refers to how AI's impact is fundamentally different from past revolutions like the steam engine.

**Q4. Which of the following is cited as a limitation of AI?**
(A) Drafting legal contracts.
(B) Diagnosing medical anomalies.
(C) Writing functional code.
(D) Emotional intelligence.
**✅ Answer: (D)**
**📖 Explanation:** The text explicitly lists problem-solving, emotional intelligence, and creativity as "areas where machines still falter."

**Q5. What is a prerequisite for the successful integration of AI in the workforce?**
(A) Banning AI in cognitive tasks.
(B) Lowering corporate taxes.
(C) Heavy investment in upskilling workers.
(D) Replacing all blue-collar jobs.
**✅ Answer: (C)**
**📖 Explanation:** The last sentence states: "Ultimately, the transition's success hinges on massive governmental and corporate investments in upskilling the current workforce."

## Passage 2: Remote Work and Corporate Culture
The abrupt pivot to remote work during the early 2020s catalyzed a permanent transformation in corporate culture. Initially viewed as a temporary emergency measure, telecommuting has proven to be highly productive for many knowledge workers, shattering the deeply entrenched management belief that physical presence in an office is a prerequisite for efficiency.

However, this paradigm shift is not devoid of challenges. While productivity metrics have remained stable or even increased, indicators of employee burnout and isolation have spiked. The blurring of lines between professional and personal life often leads to the "always-on" phenomenon, where employees feel obligated to respond to emails late into the night. Furthermore, corporate culture—the intangible glue forged through spontaneous watercooler conversations and in-person mentorship—is difficult to replicate over video calls. Consequently, progressive companies are now championing a hybrid model, attempting to marry the autonomy of remote work with the collaborative synergy of the traditional office.

**Q1. What is the author's tone toward remote work?**
(A) Overwhelmingly negative.
(B) Completely supportive.
(C) Nuanced and balanced.
(D) Highly skeptical.
**✅ Answer: (C)**
**📖 Explanation:** The author acknowledges the benefits (productivity) but also highlights serious challenges (burnout, loss of culture), indicating a balanced, nuanced view.

**Q2. What long-held corporate belief was destroyed by the remote work shift?**
(A) Employees prefer working from home.
(B) Physical office presence is necessary for efficiency.
(C) Mentorship can only happen online.
(D) Video calls are ineffective.
**✅ Answer: (B)**
**📖 Explanation:** The passage states it shattered the "entrenched management belief that physical presence in an office is a prerequisite for efficiency."

**Q3. According to the text, what is the cause of the "always-on" phenomenon?**
(A) Strict management demands.
(B) The lack of physical offices.
(C) The blurring of lines between personal and professional life.
(D) Spontaneous watercooler conversations.
**✅ Answer: (C)**
**📖 Explanation:** Direct extraction: "The blurring of lines between professional and personal life often leads to the 'always-on' phenomenon..."

**Q4. Why are companies adopting a hybrid model?**
(A) To save money on office rent.
(B) Because employees refuse to return to the office.
(C) To combine remote autonomy with in-person collaboration.
(D) To monitor employees more strictly.
**✅ Answer: (C)**
**📖 Explanation:** The final sentence says they want to "marry the autonomy of remote work with the collaborative synergy of the traditional office."

**Q5. Which word is closest in meaning to "intangible" as used in the passage?**
(A) Expensive
(B) Unclear
(C) Incapable of being touched or physically measured
(D) Irrelevant
**✅ Answer: (C)**
**📖 Explanation:** Corporate culture is described as "intangible," meaning it is a feeling or concept rather than a physical object.

## Key Strategies for Business & Tech Passages
1. **Don't panic over jargon:** You don't need a degree in Fintech or Cybersecurity to answer the questions. The answers are ALWAYS rooted in logic, not external domain knowledge.
2. **Watch the 'However':** In almost all business passages, paragraph 1 states the benefit, and paragraph 2 starts with "However" to state the risks. Inference questions usually target the risks.
3. **Pace yourself:** 200-300 words should take you exactly 90-120 seconds to read. Spend the remaining 2-3 minutes on the questions.
