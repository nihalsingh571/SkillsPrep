**Q1. Rearrange the following sentences (P, Q, R, S) to form a coherent paragraph:**

P. However, when the pandemic struck, the company was forced to shut down its retail outlets.  
Q. Over the last decade, TechCorp had primarily relied on physical stores to sell its electronics.  
R. To survive the crisis, they rapidly shifted their entire business model to e-commerce.  
S. This strategic pivot not only saved them from bankruptcy but also doubled their overall revenue.  

(A) Q, P, R, S  
(B) P, Q, R, S  
(C) Q, R, P, S  
(D) R, S, Q, P  

**✅ Answer: (A)**

**📖 Explanation:** 
1. **Find the Opening Sentence:** Sentence Q introduces the subject ("TechCorp") and establishes the historical context ("Over the last decade... physical stores"). It is independent. P starts with "However" (transition), R starts with "To survive the crisis" (what crisis?), and S starts with "This strategic pivot" (what pivot?). Thus, Q must be first. This eliminates B and D.
2. **Find a Mandatory Pair:** Sentence P introduces the problem/crisis ("pandemic struck... forced to shut down"). Sentence R provides the solution "To survive the crisis". Thus, P must precede R (PR pair).
3. **Concluding Sentence:** Sentence S refers to "This strategic pivot" which directly links back to the "shifted their entire business model" mentioned in R. So RS is a pair.
The logical flow is Q (Background) -> P (Problem) -> R (Solution) -> S (Result).

**❌ Why (B) is wrong:** Starts with a transition word ("However"), which needs preceding context.  
**❌ Why (C) is wrong:** Places R (survival) before P (the crisis they are trying to survive), breaking chronological logic.  
**❌ Why (D) is wrong:** Starts with R, which leaves "the crisis" completely unexplained.  

**📏 Rule:** Subject Introduction -> Problem -> Solution -> Result.  
**⚡ Shortcut:** Find the pronoun "This" in S. "This strategic pivot" MUST point to the "shifted their entire business model" in R. Pair RS is mandatory. Only option A has RS.  
**📊 Difficulty:** Medium  
**🏢 Company:** Cognizant GenC, Wipro NLTH

**Q2. Rearrange the following sentences (P, Q, R, S) to form a coherent paragraph:**

P. It is caused by an excessive buildup of greenhouse gases in the Earth's atmosphere.  
Q. Consequently, ice caps are melting, and sea levels are rising at an alarming rate.  
R. Global warming has become one of the most pressing environmental issues of our time.  
S. These gases trap heat from the sun, leading to a steady increase in global temperatures.  

(A) R, P, S, Q  
(B) P, S, Q, R  
(C) R, S, P, Q  
(D) S, P, R, Q  

**✅ Answer: (A)**

**📖 Explanation:** 
1. **Opening:** Sentence R introduces the main topic independently ("Global warming").
2. **Mandatory Pair 1:** Sentence P starts with "It is caused by...". What is "It"? "It" refers to Global warming from R. So RP is a pair.
3. **Mandatory Pair 2:** Sentence S starts with "These gases". Which gases? The "greenhouse gases" mentioned at the end of P. Thus, PS is a pair.
4. **Conclusion:** Sentence Q starts with "Consequently" (a result indicator), showing the final effect of the temperature increase mentioned in S. So SQ is a pair.
Flow: R (Topic) -> P (Cause) -> S (Mechanism) -> Q (Effect). 

**❌ Why (B) is wrong:** Starts with a pronoun "It" without defining the noun first.  
**❌ Why (C) is wrong:** Reverses P and S. S mentions "These gases" before P introduces "greenhouse gases".  
**❌ Why (D) is wrong:** Starts with "These gases", lacking context.  

**📏 Rule:** Noun-Pronoun Reference.  
**⚡ Shortcut:** P ends with "greenhouse gases". S starts with "These gases". The PS link is unbreakable. Only option A contains PS.  
**📊 Difficulty:** Easy  
**🏢 Company:** TCS NQT, Accenture
