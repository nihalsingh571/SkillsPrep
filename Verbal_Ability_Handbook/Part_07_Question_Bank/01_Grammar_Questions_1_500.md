## 1. Articles (Q1 - Q50)

---
**Q1. _____ Ganges is considered a holy river by Hindus.**

(A) A  
(B) An  
(C) The  
(D) No article  

**✅ Answer: (C)**

**📖 Explanation:** The definite article 'The' is used before the names of rivers, oceans, seas, and mountain ranges. 'Ganges' is a specific, unique river, so it must be preceded by 'The'. Using no article or an indefinite article is grammatically incorrect for geographical entities of this nature.

**❌ Why (A) is wrong:** 'A' is used for non-specific, singular countable nouns.  
**❌ Why (B) is wrong:** 'An' is used before vowel sounds.  
**❌ Why (D) is wrong:** Names of rivers always require the definite article.  

**📏 Rule:** Definite Article with Rivers.  
**⚡ Shortcut:** If it flows to the sea, add a 'The'.  
**📊 Difficulty:** Easy  
**🏢 Company:** Wipro NLTH, Capgemini  
---

**Q2. He is _____ heir to a massive fortune.**

(A) a  
(B) an  
(C) the  
(D) no article  

**✅ Answer: (B)**

**📖 Explanation:** Although 'heir' starts with the consonant letter 'h', the 'h' is silent. The word is pronounced as "air", which begins with a vowel sound. Therefore, the indefinite article 'an' must be used instead of 'a'.

**❌ Why (A) is wrong:** 'A' is used before consonant sounds, but 'heir' starts with a vowel sound.  
**❌ Why (C) is wrong:** 'The' is used for specific nouns; here it is a general statement about his status.  
**❌ Why (D) is wrong:** A singular countable noun requires an article.  

**📏 Rule:** Vowel Sound Rule (Silent H).  
**⚡ Shortcut:** Trust your ears, not your eyes. Read it out loud.  
**📊 Difficulty:** Medium  
**🏢 Company:** TCS NQT, Cognizant

## 2. Subject-Verb Agreement (Q51 - Q150)

---
**Q51. The manager, as well as his team members, _____ scheduled to attend the conference.**

(A) is  
(B) are  
(C) have  
(D) has  

**✅ Answer: (A)**

**📖 Explanation:** When two subjects are connected by phrases like 'as well as', 'along with', 'together with', or 'in addition to', the verb must agree with the FIRST subject. Here, the first subject is 'The manager' (singular). Therefore, the singular verb 'is' is required.

**❌ Why (B) is wrong:** 'Are' would be used if the subjects were connected by 'and'.  
**❌ Why (C) is wrong:** 'Have' is plural and grammatically doesn't fit the passive voice structure 'scheduled'.  
**❌ Why (D) is wrong:** 'Has scheduled' changes the meaning to active voice, implying the manager scheduled someone else.  

**📏 Rule:** The "As Well As" Rule.  
**⚡ Shortcut:** Ignore everything between the commas. "The manager... is scheduled."  
**📊 Difficulty:** Hard  
**🏢 Company:** Accenture, Infosys  
---

**Q52. Neither the students nor the teacher _____ aware of the changing exam pattern.**

(A) was  
(B) were  
(C) has  
(D) have  

**✅ Answer: (A)**

**📖 Explanation:** In "Neither... nor" and "Either... or" constructions, the verb must agree with the subject closest to it (the Rule of Proximity). The subject closest to the verb is 'the teacher', which is singular. Thus, the singular verb 'was' is correct.

**❌ Why (B) is wrong:** 'Were' is plural and would only be used if 'students' was placed closest to the verb.  
**❌ Why (C) is wrong:** 'Has aware' is grammatically invalid.  
**❌ Why (D) is wrong:** 'Have' is plural and invalid with the adjective 'aware'.  

**📏 Rule:** Rule of Proximity.  
**⚡ Shortcut:** Look at the noun right next to the blank. Teacher = singular = was.  
**📊 Difficulty:** Medium  
**🏢 Company:** IBM, TCS NQT

## 3. Tenses (Q151 - Q250)

---
**Q151. By the time the fire department arrived, the building _____ down.**

(A) burnt  
(B) was burning  
(C) has burnt  
(D) had burnt  

**✅ Answer: (D)**

**📖 Explanation:** When two actions occur in the past, the action that happens FIRST must be in the Past Perfect tense (had + V3), and the action that happens LATER must be in the Simple Past tense. The building burned down first, and then the fire department arrived.

**❌ Why (A) is wrong:** 'Burnt' is simple past. Both actions cannot be in simple past if one clearly preceded the other.  
**❌ Why (B) is wrong:** 'Was burning' implies an ongoing action, but 'down' implies completion.  
**❌ Why (C) is wrong:** 'Has burnt' is present perfect, which cannot mix with the past tense 'arrived'.  

**📏 Rule:** Double Past Action Rule (Past Perfect vs Simple Past).  
**⚡ Shortcut:** "By the time [Simple Past], [Past Perfect]."  
**📊 Difficulty:** Hard  
**🏢 Company:** Capgemini, Accenture
