## Passage 1: Artificial Intelligence in Healthcare (Q1 - Q10)

**Read the following passage carefully and answer the questions that follow:**

The integration of Artificial Intelligence (AI) into healthcare is no longer a distant futuristic concept; it is an active paradigm shift. AI algorithms, powered by deep learning and neural networks, have demonstrated an unprecedented ability to analyze complex medical data. For instance, diagnostic AI systems can now examine radiological images with a level of precision that rivals, and sometimes surpasses, seasoned human radiologists. This reduces human error, especially in early-stage tumor detection where microscopic anomalies are easily overlooked. 

However, the widespread deployment of AI in medicine is not devoid of challenges. The primary obstacle remains data privacy and security. AI systems require massive datasets—often comprising sensitive patient health records—to train effectively. Ensuring that these datasets are anonymized and protected against cyber breaches is paramount. Furthermore, there is the ethical dilemma of "algorithmic bias." If the AI is trained on data heavily skewed towards a specific demographic, its diagnostic accuracy for minority populations plummets, potentially exacerbating existing healthcare inequalities.

Despite these hurdles, the consensus among medical technologists is that AI will not replace doctors but rather augment them. The future of healthcare lies in a collaborative model where AI handles data-heavy diagnostic tasks, freeing physicians to focus on holistic patient care, empathy, and complex decision-making.

---
**Q1. According to the passage, what is the primary obstacle to deploying AI in healthcare?**

(A) The inability of AI to match human radiologists in detecting microscopic anomalies.  
(B) The ethical dilemma of replacing human doctors entirely.  
(C) Concerns regarding the privacy and security of sensitive patient data.  
(D) The lack of computing power required to train deep learning models.  

**✅ Answer: (C)**

**📖 Explanation:** The answer is explicitly stated in the second paragraph: "The primary obstacle remains data privacy and security." The passage explains that AI needs massive datasets of sensitive patient records, making privacy a critical issue. 

**❌ Why (A) is wrong:** The passage states the opposite; AI rivals or surpasses human radiologists.  
**❌ Why (B) is wrong:** The passage says the consensus is that AI will *not* replace doctors.  
**❌ Why (D) is wrong:** Computing power is not mentioned as a challenge in the passage.  

**📏 Rule:** Fact-Based RC Question (Direct Scan).  
**⚡ Shortcut:** Scan for keywords "primary obstacle". The sentence immediately after gives the answer.  
**📊 Difficulty:** Easy  
**🏢 Company:** Infosys, Wipro  
---

**Q2. The author's attitude towards the integration of AI in healthcare can best be described as:**

(A) Highly skeptical and pessimistic.  
(B) Cautiously optimistic.  
(C) Completely neutral.  
(D) Blindly enthusiastic.  

**✅ Answer: (B)**

**📖 Explanation:** The author acknowledges the massive benefits of AI (precision, reducing human error, augmenting doctors) but also dedicates an entire paragraph to the severe challenges (data privacy, algorithmic bias). This balanced view—acknowledging the bright future while addressing the hurdles—is best described as "cautiously optimistic."

**❌ Why (A) is wrong:** The author highlights many positives, so they are not highly pessimistic.  
**❌ Why (C) is wrong:** The author clearly believes AI is beneficial ("unprecedented ability", "augment them"), showing optimism, not pure neutrality.  
**❌ Why (D) is wrong:** The author explicitly lists major hurdles, showing they are not blindly enthusiastic.  

**📏 Rule:** Tone/Attitude Inference Question.  
**⚡ Shortcut:** If a passage has both PROS and CONS but concludes positively, the tone is almost always "Cautiously Optimistic".  
**📊 Difficulty:** Medium  
**🏢 Company:** TCS NQT, Cognizant
