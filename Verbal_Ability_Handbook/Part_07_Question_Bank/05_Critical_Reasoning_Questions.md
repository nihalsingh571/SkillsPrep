## 1. Assumption Questions (Q1 - Q75)

---
**Q1. Statement:** To boost productivity, the company has decided to allow all employees to work from home two days a week.

**Which of the following is an ASSUMPTION made by the company in taking this decision?**

(A) Employees prefer working from home over coming to the office.  
(B) The company's competitors already offer remote work options.  
(C) Working from home does not negatively impact the employees' ability to complete their tasks.  
(D) The company will save money on electricity and office supplies.  

**✅ Answer: (C)**

**📖 Explanation:** An assumption is an unstated premise that must be true for the conclusion to hold. The company's goal is to "boost productivity" by allowing work from home. If working from home severely hindered an employee's ability to complete tasks, productivity would drop, destroying the company's goal. Therefore, the company MUST assume that WFH does not negatively impact task completion. Use the Negation Test: If we negate (C) to "WFH negatively impacts task completion," the company's decision completely falls apart.

**❌ Why (A) is wrong:** Even if employees don't prefer it, the company could still implement it for productivity. Employee preference is not the core assumption for boosting productivity.  
**❌ Why (B) is wrong:** Competitors' policies are irrelevant to this specific company's internal productivity goal.  
**❌ Why (D) is wrong:** Saving money might be a side benefit, but the stated goal is "to boost productivity," not to cut costs.  

**📏 Rule:** The Negation Test for Assumptions.  
**⚡ Shortcut:** If reversing the option destroys the statement's logic, that option is the correct assumption.  
**📊 Difficulty:** Hard  
**🏢 Company:** Infosys, Accenture

## 4. Weaken Questions (Q201 - Q250)

---
**Q201. Statement:** Switching to electric vehicles (EVs) is the most effective way for our city to reduce overall carbon emissions, because EVs produce zero tailpipe emissions while driving.

**Which of the following, if true, most seriously WEAKENS the argument above?**

(A) Electric vehicles are currently more expensive to purchase than traditional gasoline cars.  
(B) The city's electricity grid is powered entirely by heavily polluting coal power plants.  
(C) It takes several hours to fully charge an electric vehicle battery.  
(D) Many residents of the city prefer walking or cycling for short commutes.  

**✅ Answer: (B)**

**📖 Explanation:** To weaken an argument, we must find a fact that breaks the link between the premise (EVs have zero tailpipe emissions) and the conclusion (EVs will reduce OVERALL carbon emissions). If the electricity used to charge the "zero emission" EVs comes from highly polluting coal plants (Option B), then the overall emissions might not decrease at all; the pollution is just shifted from the car to the power plant. This directly attacks the conclusion.

**❌ Why (A) is wrong:** Cost affects adoption rate, but doesn't weaken the core claim about carbon emissions.  
**❌ Why (C) is wrong:** Charging time is an inconvenience but has nothing to do with carbon emissions.  
**❌ Why (D) is wrong:** While good for the environment, it doesn't weaken the specific claim about EVs compared to gasoline cars.  

**📏 Rule:** Attack the Gap (Premise to Conclusion gap).  
**⚡ Shortcut:** Look for the option that says "Actually, the proposed solution causes the same exact problem in a different way."  
**📊 Difficulty:** Medium  
**🏢 Company:** IBM, Cognizant
