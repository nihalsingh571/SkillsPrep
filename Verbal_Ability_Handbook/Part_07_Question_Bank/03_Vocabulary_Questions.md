## 1. Synonyms (Q1001 - Q1100)

---
**Q1001. Choose the synonym for the word: MITIGATE**

(A) Aggravate  
(B) Alleviate  
(C) Interrogate  
(D) Penetrate  

**✅ Answer: (B)**

**📖 Explanation:** The word 'mitigate' means to make something less severe, harmful, or painful. For example, "The medicine helped mitigate the pain." 'Alleviate' has the exact same meaning (to relieve or lessen). Both are high-frequency words in GRE and placement level exams.

**❌ Why (A) is wrong:** 'Aggravate' means to make worse; it is the antonym of mitigate.  
**❌ Why (C) is wrong:** 'Interrogate' means to question formally.  
**❌ Why (D) is wrong:** 'Penetrate' means to pierce or go into something.  

**📏 Rule:** Vocabulary - Synonyms.  
**⚡ Shortcut:** Root word trick: 'Miti-' means mild. Making something milder is to alleviate it.  
**📊 Difficulty:** Medium  
**🏢 Company:** TCS NQT, Wipro NLTH

## 2. Antonyms (Q1101 - Q1200)

---
**Q1101. Choose the antonym for the word: EPHEMERAL**

(A) Transient  
(B) Permanent  
(C) Mystical  
(D) Fragile  

**✅ Answer: (B)**

**📖 Explanation:** 'Ephemeral' refers to something that lasts for a very short time, fleeting, or short-lived (e.g., ephemeral fame). The exact opposite of something short-lived is something that lasts forever, which is 'Permanent'. 

**❌ Why (A) is wrong:** 'Transient' is a synonym for ephemeral, not an antonym.  
**❌ Why (C) is wrong:** 'Mystical' refers to spiritual mystery, completely unrelated.  
**❌ Why (D) is wrong:** 'Fragile' means easily broken, which is unrelated to time duration.  

**📏 Rule:** Vocabulary - Antonyms.  
**⚡ Shortcut:** Ephemeral = E-frame-real (A frame that vanishes real quick). Opposite is Permanent.  
**📊 Difficulty:** Hard  
**🏢 Company:** Cognizant GenC, Infosys

## 3. Idioms and Phrases (Q1401 - Q1500)

---
**Q1401. What does the idiom "Bite the bullet" mean in the following sentence?**  
*Facing massive losses, the startup had to bite the bullet and lay off half its staff.*

(A) To act violently  
(B) To endure a painful or difficult situation bravely  
(C) To make a foolish mistake  
(D) To attack the enemy  

**✅ Answer: (B)**

**📖 Explanation:** The idiom "Bite the bullet" originated from the days before anesthesia, when wounded soldiers were given a bullet to bite on during surgery to help them endure the pain. Thus, it means to face a difficult, unpleasant, or painful situation with courage and resolve, as avoiding it is impossible.

**❌ Why (A) is wrong:** It doesn't relate to violence.  
**❌ Why (C) is wrong:** Biting a bullet sounds like a mistake, but it's actually an act of endurance.  
**❌ Why (D) is wrong:** It is a defensive act of endurance, not an offensive attack.  

**📏 Rule:** Idiomatic Expression context.  
**⚡ Shortcut:** Bite bullet = bear pain.  
**📊 Difficulty:** Easy  
**🏢 Company:** Accenture, Capgemini
