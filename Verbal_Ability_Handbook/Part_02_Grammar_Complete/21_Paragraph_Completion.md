## 21.1 What is Paragraph Completion?

In this question type, you are given a short paragraph with the final sentence (or sometimes a middle sentence) missing. You must choose the option that logically completes the paragraph.

While Para Jumbles test **Cohesion** (grammatical links like pronouns), Paragraph Completion tests **Coherence** (logical flow of ideas). 

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Placement Relevance:** This is considered one of the hardest verbal sections in **CAT, Infosys, and high-tier consulting exams**. It requires reading comprehension skills applied at a micro-level.

---

## 21.2 The "B.O.A.R." Elimination Strategy

You will rarely find the right answer by just reading. You must eliminate the three wrong answers. Use the B.O.A.R. acronym to destroy wrong options:

*   **B - Broad (Too General):** The paragraph is about electric cars in India. The option talks about global warming generally. (Reject).
*   **O - Out of Scope (Irrelevant):** The paragraph is about software testing. The option talks about hardware manufacturing. (Reject).
*   **A - Alien Tone (Contradiction):** The paragraph is praising a new technology. The option suddenly criticizes it without a transition word like 'however'. (Reject).
*   **R - Repetitive:** The option just repeats what was already said in sentence 2 using different words. A concluding sentence must *push the idea forward* or *summarize*, not just repeat a premise. (Reject).

---

## 21.3 Structural Clues for the Missing Sentence

**If the missing sentence is at the END:**
*   It should serve as a conclusion or a logical consequence of the previous sentences.
*   It should NOT introduce a completely new topic that requires further explanation.

**If the missing sentence is in the MIDDLE:**
*   It must act as a bridge. It must logically follow the sentence before it, AND logically lead into the sentence after it.

## 21.4 Master Practice Bank

**Q1. Complete the paragraph:**
The shift to remote work during the pandemic proved that employees do not need to be in a physical office to be productive. Many companies reported an increase in overall output and a decrease in operational costs. However, this transition was not without its drawbacks. _______________.

(A) Furthermore, employees saved a lot of money and time on daily commutes.
(B) Remote work allowed companies to hire global talent without geographical restrictions.
(C) Managers struggled to maintain team cohesion and monitor the mental well-being of isolated employees.
(D) The cost of renting office spaces plummeted in major metropolitan cities.

**✅ Answer: (C)**
**📖 Explanation:** The sentence before the blank states: "However, this transition was not without its drawbacks." Therefore, the blank MUST provide an example of a drawback. Options A, B, and D are all positive benefits. Option C highlights a negative drawback (managers struggling, isolated employees).
**Elimination:** A (Alien Tone - Positive), B (Alien Tone - Positive), D (Alien Tone - Positive).
**Difficulty:** Easy
**Company:** Infosys

**Q2. Complete the paragraph:**
Artificial Intelligence is rapidly transforming the healthcare industry. Algorithms can now analyze medical images with a higher accuracy rate than human radiologists. AI-driven chatbots are triaging patients, and predictive models are forecasting disease outbreaks. _______________.

(A) Doctors will soon be completely replaced by robots in all surgical procedures.
(B) The automotive industry is also seeing massive disruptions due to autonomous driving algorithms.
(C) Despite these advancements, the ethical implications of data privacy and algorithmic bias remain significant hurdles.
(D) These technological interventions are ultimately leading to faster diagnoses and more personalized patient care.

**✅ Answer: (D)**
**📖 Explanation:** The paragraph lists three positive examples of AI in healthcare. It needs a concluding sentence that summarizes the overall impact of these examples. Option D perfectly summarizes the result: faster diagnosis and personalized care.
**Elimination:** A (Too extreme/broad claim). B (Out of scope - automotive industry). C (Alien Tone - introduces a negative without a transition word like 'however' preceding it. If the blank started with 'However', C would be a good fit, but it doesn't).
**Difficulty:** Medium
**Company:** TCS NQT

**Q3. Complete the paragraph:**
In agile software development, teams work in short sprints, usually lasting two weeks. At the end of each sprint, a potentially shippable product increment is delivered. _______________. This allows the team to continuously adapt to changing client requirements.

(A) The waterfall model, on the other hand, requires all planning to be done upfront.
(B) The stakeholders then review this increment and provide immediate feedback.
(C) Developers often find these two-week deadlines to be highly stressful.
(D) The project manager creates a Gantt chart for the next year.

**✅ Answer: (B)**
**📖 Explanation:** This is a middle-blank question. It must connect the delivery of the increment to the team's ability to adapt. How do they adapt? They get feedback. Option B acts as the perfect bridge: Increment is delivered -> Stakeholders give feedback -> Team adapts.
**Elimination:** A (Out of scope - shifts focus to Waterfall). C (Breaks the logical flow of process description). D (Contradicts Agile methodology of short-term planning).
**Difficulty:** Hard
**Company:** Wipro

**Q4. Complete the paragraph:**
Cryptocurrencies operate on decentralized networks based on blockchain technology. Unlike traditional fiat currencies issued by central banks, crypto relies on cryptographic proofs to secure transactions. Because no single entity controls the network, it is highly resistant to censorship. _______________.

(A) Therefore, transferring money across international borders is faster and cheaper.
(B) This lack of central authority, however, makes it highly volatile and difficult to regulate.
(C) Bitcoin was the first cryptocurrency, created by an anonymous person named Satoshi Nakamoto.
(D) Many people invest in stocks and bonds to secure their financial future.

**✅ Answer: (B)**
**📖 Explanation:** The paragraph discusses the decentralized, censorship-resistant nature of crypto. A good conclusion often presents the flip side or a direct consequence. Option B directly references "This lack of central authority" (linking to "no single entity controls") and introduces the logical consequence (volatility/regulation difficulty).
**Elimination:** A (A bit broad, doesn't directly flow from "censorship resistance"). C (Out of scope - historical fact not related to the technical mechanics discussed). D (Out of scope entirely).
**Difficulty:** Medium
**Company:** Accenture

**Q5. Complete the paragraph:**
A true leader does not simply command their subordinates; they inspire them. While a manager focuses on assigning tasks and ensuring deadlines are met, a leader focuses on the vision. _______________.

(A) They empower their team members to take ownership of their work and grow professionally.
(B) Managers are usually paid higher salaries than regular team members.
(C) The company's stock price relies heavily on the quarterly earnings report.
(D) Therefore, meeting deadlines is the most critical aspect of corporate success.

**✅ Answer: (A)**
**📖 Explanation:** The paragraph contrasts managers (tasks/deadlines) with leaders (vision/inspiration). The final sentence needs to elaborate on what a leader does. Option A perfectly aligns with "inspiring" and "focusing on vision" by describing empowerment and growth.
**Elimination:** B (Out of scope). C (Out of scope). D (Contradicts the main idea that vision/leadership is more than just deadlines).
**Difficulty:** Easy
**Company:** Cognizant
