## 25.1 What is One Word Substitution?

This tests your exact vocabulary knowledge. You are given a phrase and must replace it with a single word. It is highly prevalent in **TCS NQT and SSC-level exams**.

## 25.2 People, Beliefs, and Behavior

*   **Altruist:** A person who unselfishly cares for others.
*   **Misanthrope:** A person who hates mankind.
*   **Misogynist:** A person who hates women.
*   **Philanthropist:** A lover of mankind (often expressed through charity).
*   **Polyglot:** A person who speaks many languages fluently.
*   **Introvert:** A person focused on their inner thoughts.
*   **Extrovert:** A person focused on the external world/socializing.
*   **Ambivert:** A person who is both introverted and extroverted.
*   **Optimist:** One who looks at the bright side of things.
*   **Pessimist:** One who looks at the dark side of things.
*   **Atheist:** One who does not believe in God.
*   **Agnostic:** One who believes it is impossible to know if God exists.
*   **Gullible:** Easily persuaded to believe something.
*   **Fastidious:** Very attentive to and concerned about accuracy and detail (hard to please).
*   **Stoic:** A person who can endure pain or hardship without showing their feelings.
*   **Hypocrite:** One who pretends to be what they are not.

## 25.3 Places and Collections

*   **Apiary:** A place where bees are kept. (Root: Apis = bee).
*   **Aviary:** A place where birds are kept. (Root: Avis = bird).
*   **Aquarium:** A tank for fish/water plants.
*   **Sanatorium:** A place for the care of the sick/convalescent.
*   **Arsenal:** A place where weapons and ammunition are stored.
*   **Granary:** A storehouse for threshed grain.
*   **Orphanage:** A place where orphans are housed.
*   **Archives:** A place where public/historical records are kept.

---

## 25.4 The "Cracies" (Forms of Government)

*   **Democracy:** Government by the people. (Demos = people).
*   **Autocracy:** Government by one person with absolute power.
*   **Aristocracy:** Government by the nobility/highest social class.
*   **Plutocracy:** Government by the wealthy. (Ploutos = wealth).
*   **Oligarchy:** Government by a small group of people.
*   **Bureaucracy:** Government by unelected state officials.
*   **Theocracy:** Government by religious leaders.
*   **Anarchy:** Absence of government/total disorder.

---

## 25.5 The "Phobias" (Fears)

*   **Acrophobia:** Fear of heights.
*   **Claustrophobia:** Fear of enclosed/confined spaces.
*   **Agoraphobia:** Fear of open or public places.
*   **Hydrophobia:** Fear of water (often associated with rabies).
*   **Pyrophobia:** Fear of fire.
*   **Xenophobia:** Fear or hatred of foreigners/strangers.

---

## 25.6 The "Ologies" (Fields of Study)

*   **Etymology:** The study of the origin of words. (Don't confuse with Entomology).
*   **Entomology:** The study of insects.
*   **Ornithology:** The study of birds.
*   **Pathology:** The study of diseases.
*   **Anthropology:** The study of human societies and cultures.
*   **Philology:** The study of languages and written records.

## 25.7 Practice Questions

**Q1. A person who speaks many languages is called a:**
(A) Linguist
(B) Bilingual
(C) Polyglot
(D) Grammarian

**✅ Answer: (C)**
**📖 Explanation:** "Polyglot" specifically means someone who knows or uses several languages. "Linguist" is a scientist who studies the structure of language (though it can casually mean polyglot, polyglot is the exact term). "Bilingual" is only two languages.
**Rule:** People terms.
**Difficulty:** Medium
**Company:** TCS

**Q2. A government by the wealthy class is known as:**
(A) Aristocracy
(B) Plutocracy
(C) Oligarchy
(D) Autocracy

**✅ Answer: (B)**
**📖 Explanation:** Ploutos = wealth. Plutocracy = rule by the wealthy. Aristocracy = rule by nobles. Oligarchy = rule by a few.
**Rule:** Cracies.
**Difficulty:** Medium
**Company:** Infosys

**Q3. One who hates mankind:**
(A) Philanthropist
(B) Misogynist
(C) Misanthrope
(D) Introvert

**✅ Answer: (C)**
**📖 Explanation:** "Misein" (to hate) + "Anthropos" (mankind) = Misanthrope. A philanthropist loves mankind. A misogynist hates women.
**Rule:** People terms.
**Difficulty:** Easy
**Company:** Wipro

**Q4. The study of the origin and history of words:**
(A) Entomology
(B) Etymology
(C) Epistemology
(D) Anthropology

**✅ Answer: (B)**
**📖 Explanation:** Etymology is the study of words. Entomology is the study of insects. Epistemology is the study of knowledge.
**Rule:** Ologies.
**Difficulty:** Hard
**Company:** Accenture

**Q5. A place where bees are kept is called an:**
(A) Aviary
(B) Apiary
(C) Aquarium
(D) Arsenal

**✅ Answer: (B)**
**📖 Explanation:** Apiary is for bees (Apis). Aviary is for birds (Avis).
**Rule:** Places.
**Difficulty:** Easy
**Company:** Cognizant
