## 20.1 The "Cracking the Code" 4-Step Method

Para Jumbles (Sentence Rearrangement) are guaranteed in **TCS, Cognizant, and Infosys**. You do NOT need to understand the whole paragraph to solve it. You just need to find the logical links.

### Step 1: Find the Opening Sentence (The Independent Anchor)
The first sentence introduces the topic. It MUST stand alone. 
*   **Rule out:** Sentences starting with pronouns (He, She, They, It). *Exception: "It" as a dummy subject ("It is a truth universally acknowledged").*
*   **Rule out:** Sentences starting with transition words (However, Therefore, Also, Consequently).
*   **Look for:** Nouns, full names, definitions, broad statements, time markers ("In 1990...").

### Step 2: Find Mandatory Pairs (The Magic Links)
This is the most important step. Look for two sentences that MUST go together.
*   **The Pronoun Link:** If Sentence A says "Mr. Sharma", and Sentence C says "He", A must come before C.
*   **The Article Link:** If Sentence B says "**A** new project", and Sentence D says "**The** project", B must come before D (Indefinite to Definite).
*   **The Transition Link:** If Sentence A says "Sales increased." and Sentence B says "However, profits fell.", A -> B is a mandatory pair of contrast.
*   **The Full Form / Acronym Link:** "Artificial Intelligence (AI)" comes before just "AI".

### Step 3: Find the Closing Sentence
The closing sentence concludes the thought. 
*   **Look for:** "Thus", "Therefore", "Ultimately", "Finally", "In conclusion", or a sentence that summarizes the entire paragraph.

### Step 4: Arrange and Eliminate Options
Once you have your opening sentence and one mandatory pair, look at the 4 options. Eliminate any option that doesn't have your opening sentence first, and your mandatory pair together.

## 20.2 The 5 Types of Logical Flow

When arranging, check if the sentences follow a standard paragraph structure:
1.  **Chronological:** Past -> Present -> Future (Dates are huge clues).
2.  **General to Specific:** Broad topic -> Specific example. (e.g., "Pollution is bad." -> "In Delhi, smog levels are high.")
3.  **Cause and Effect:** Action -> Result. (e.g., "The server crashed." -> "Therefore, data was lost.")
4.  **Problem and Solution:** (e.g., "Traffic is a nightmare." -> "Building more metros is the answer.")
5.  **Idea and Explanation:** Concept -> Elaboration.

## 20.3 Master Practice Bank

**Q1. Arrange the sentences in the correct order:**
A. He was looking forward to the new challenges the role would bring.
B. Ramesh recently joined the software division of a multinational company.
C. This was a significant step up from his previous job at a local startup.
D. Therefore, he spent his weekends studying the company's proprietary tech stack.

(A) B C A D
(B) B A C D
(C) C B A D
(D) A B C D

**✅ Answer: (A)**
**📖 Explanation:** 
*   **Step 1 (Opener):** (B) introduces the noun "Ramesh" and the main event (joining a company). (A) starts with "He", (C) starts with "This", (D) starts with "Therefore". B is the opener.
*   **Step 2 (Pairs):** "This" in (C) refers to joining the multinational company mentioned in (B). So, B -> C is a mandatory pair. (A) talks about looking forward to challenges. (D) says "Therefore" he studied. A -> D is a cause-and-effect pair.
*   **Order:** B -> C -> A -> D.
**Rule:** Pronoun and Demonstrative pronoun ("This") links.
**Difficulty:** Easy
**Company:** TCS NQT

**Q2. Arrange the sentences in the correct order:**
A. However, this convenience comes at a significant cost to privacy.
B. Smart home devices have become increasingly popular in urban households.
C. These devices constantly listen and record data to function efficiently.
D. They offer unparalleled convenience by automating daily tasks.

(A) B D A C
(B) B D C A
(C) D B A C
(D) B A D C

**✅ Answer: (A)**
**📖 Explanation:**
*   **Step 1 (Opener):** (B) introduces the main noun "Smart home devices" broadly.
*   **Step 2 (Pairs):** (D) says "They offer... convenience". "They" refers to devices. So B -> D. (A) says "However, THIS convenience...". This directly references the convenience mentioned in (D). So D -> A is a rock-solid mandatory pair. (C) explains how the privacy is lost (constantly listening), elaborating on (A).
*   **Order:** B -> D -> A -> C.
**Rule:** Keyword linking ("convenience" to "this convenience").
**Difficulty:** Medium
**Company:** Infosys

**Q3. Arrange the sentences in the correct order:**
A. The company decided to switch to a cloud-based infrastructure.
B. Consequently, they saw a 40% reduction in server maintenance costs within a year.
C. For years, they had struggled with maintaining physical servers which were prone to failure.
D. This decision required a massive initial investment and training for the IT team.

(A) A D C B
(B) C A D B
(C) C A B D
(D) A C D B

**✅ Answer: (B)**
**📖 Explanation:**
*   **Step 1 (Opener):** (C) gives the historical context/problem ("For years, they had struggled..."). It sets up the reason for the action.
*   **Step 2 (Pairs):** Because of the struggle in (C), they took the action in (A): decided to switch. C -> A. (D) says "This decision" which points directly back to the decision made in (A). A -> D is a mandatory pair. (B) starts with "Consequently" (result), which summarizes the final outcome of the decision.
*   **Order:** C -> A -> D -> B.
**Rule:** Problem -> Solution -> Execution -> Result flow.
**Difficulty:** Medium
**Company:** Wipro

**Q4. Arrange the sentences in the correct order:**
A. To mitigate this, engineers are developing advanced cooling systems.
B. Quantum computers generate an immense amount of heat due to their processing power.
C. If not controlled, this heat can destroy the delicate qubits essential for computation.
D. These systems use liquid helium to keep temperatures near absolute zero.

(A) B C A D
(B) B A C D
(C) A B C D
(D) B C D A

**✅ Answer: (A)**
**📖 Explanation:**
*   **Opener:** (B) introduces Quantum computers and the problem (heat).
*   **Pairs:** (C) elaborates on the problem mentioned in (B) ("this heat"). B -> C. (A) introduces the solution ("To mitigate this" - referring to the destruction in C). C -> A. (D) explains the systems mentioned in (A) ("These systems"). A -> D.
*   **Order:** B -> C -> A -> D.
**Rule:** Noun -> Pronoun linking.
**Difficulty:** Hard
**Company:** Accenture

**Q5. Arrange the sentences in the correct order:**
A. Firstly, it requires parsing the text into tokens.
B. Natural Language Processing (NLP) allows computers to understand human language.
C. Finally, the model generates an appropriate response based on the analysis.
D. Secondly, these tokens are analyzed for grammatical structure and sentiment.
E. However, this process involves several complex steps.

(A) B E A D C
(B) B A D C E
(C) E B A D C
(D) B E D A C

**✅ Answer: (A)**
**📖 Explanation:**
*   **Opener:** (B) introduces the broad topic NLP.
*   **Pairs:** (E) acts as a bridge: "this process involves several complex steps". What steps? (A) Firstly... (D) Secondly... (C) Finally...
*   **Order:** B -> E -> A -> D -> C.
**Rule:** Enumeration (Firstly, Secondly, Finally).
**Difficulty:** Easy
**Company:** Cognizant
