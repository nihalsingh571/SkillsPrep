## 26.1 The Power of Etymology

You cannot memorize all 170,000 words in the English dictionary. But if you memorize 100 Latin and Greek roots, you can unlock the meaning of 10,000+ words. This is the ultimate "hack" for the Reading Comprehension and Synonym/Antonym sections in **TCS, Infosys, and CAT**.

A word is built like this:
`PREFIX` (Changes direction/negation) + `ROOT` (Core meaning) + `SUFFIX` (Changes part of speech).

*Example:* **Incredible**
*   `In-` (Not) + `Cred` (Believe) + `-ible` (Able to be) = Not able to be believed (So amazing it's hard to believe).

## 26.2 High-Value Root Words (The Core)

### 1. BENE / BON (Good, Well)
*   **Benefit:** A good result.
*   **Benefactor:** One who does good (gives money).
*   **Benevolent:** Wishing good things for others (kind).
*   **Benign:** Gentle, not harmful (a benign tumor).
*   **Bonus:** Something good given extra.

### 2. MAL (Bad, Evil)
*   **Malice:** Desire to cause harm.
*   **Malevolent:** Wishing evil on others.
*   **Malfunction:** To function badly.
*   **Malignant:** Harmful, cancerous.
*   **Malware:** Malicious software.

### 3. DICT (To Say, To Speak)
*   **Dictate:** To speak aloud for someone to write down, or to command.
*   **Predict:** To say what will happen in the future (Pre = before).
*   **Contradict:** To speak against (Contra = against).
*   **Verdict:** A truthful saying (Ver = truth), a jury's decision.
*   **Benediction:** A good saying (A blessing).

### 4. SCRIB / SCRIPT (To Write)
*   **Scribble:** To write quickly or messily.
*   **Describe:** To write down features.
*   **Prescription:** Written before (by a doctor).
*   **Inscribe:** To write into/onto something.
*   **Transcript:** A written copy.

### 5. PORT (To Carry)
*   **Portable:** Able to be carried.
*   **Transport:** To carry across (Trans = across).
*   **Import:** To carry into a country.
*   **Export:** To carry out of a country.
*   **Portfolio:** A case for carrying loose papers.

### 6. VIS / VID (To See)
*   **Visible:** Able to be seen.
*   **Vision:** The ability to see.
*   **Video:** The recording of visual images.
*   **Supervise:** To oversee (Super = over).
*   **Evidence:** Out of what can be seen (E = out).

### 7. CHRON (Time)
*   **Chronological:** Arranged in order of time.
*   **Chronic:** Lasting a long time (chronic pain).
*   **Synchronize:** To make things happen at the same time (Syn = same).

### 8. PATH (Feeling, Disease)
*   **Sympathy:** Feeling the same as someone (Sym = same).
*   **Apathy:** Lack of feeling (A = without).
*   **Antipathy:** Strong dislike/feeling against.
*   **Pathology:** The study of diseases.

### 9. CRED (To Believe)
*   **Credible:** Believable.
*   **Incredible:** Unbelievable.
*   **Credulous:** Too ready to believe (gullible).
*   **Credentials:** Documents proving belief/qualification.

### 10. VOC / VOK (Voice, Call)
*   **Vocal:** Relating to the voice.
*   **Invoke:** To call upon.
*   **Revoke:** To call back/cancel (Re = back).
*   **Provoke:** To call forth anger (Pro = forward).

## 26.3 Essential Prefixes (The Modifiers)

*   **A- / An- :** Without (Apathy, Anonymous).
*   **Anti- / Contra- :** Against (Antivirus, Contradict).
*   **De- :** Down, away (Descend, Depress).
*   **Ex- :** Out (Export, Exit).
*   **In- / Im- / Il- / Ir- :** Not (Invisible, Impossible, Illegal, Irregular).
*   **Pre- :** Before (Predict).
*   **Post- :** After (Postpone).
*   **Re- :** Again, back (Rewrite, Return).
*   **Sub- :** Under (Submarine).

---

## 26.4 Essential Suffixes (The Part of Speech Changers)

*   **-tion / -sion:** Makes a Noun (Action, Vision).
*   **-ity / -ness:** Makes a Noun indicating a state (Clarity, Darkness).
*   **-ify / -ize:** Makes a Verb meaning to become (Purify, Modernize).
*   **-ous / -ful:** Makes an Adjective meaning full of (Joyous, Beautiful).
*   **-ly:** Makes an Adverb (Quickly).

## 26.5 Practice Questions (Deduction using Roots)

**Q1. Using your knowledge of roots, what does the word "Malevolent" mean?**
(A) Wishing to do good to others
(B) Speaking loudly
(C) Having or showing a wish to do evil to others
(D) Unable to write

**✅ Answer: (C)**
**📖 Explanation:** `Mal-` means bad or evil. `Vol-` means will or wish. `Malevolent` means wishing evil.
**Rule:** Root MAL.
**Difficulty:** Medium
**Company:** Infosys

**Q2. If "Spect" means "to look", what does "Retrospect" mean?**
(A) Looking forward to the future
(B) Looking back on past events
(C) Looking at something very closely
(D) Looking away from a problem

**✅ Answer: (B)**
**📖 Explanation:** `Retro-` means backwards/past. `Spect` means look. Therefore, retrospect means looking back.
**Rule:** Prefix Retro + Root Spect.
**Difficulty:** Easy
**Company:** TCS

**Q3. A "Philanthropist" is someone who:**
(A) Hates mankind
(B) Loves and helps mankind
(C) Studies diseases
(D) Collects stamps

**✅ Answer: (B)**
**📖 Explanation:** `Phil-` means love. `Anthropos` means mankind.
**Rule:** Root Phil + Anthropos.
**Difficulty:** Medium
**Company:** Wipro

**Q4. The word "Dictator" implies someone who:**
(A) Writes all the laws
(B) Sees all the actions of the state
(C) Has absolute authority and gives spoken commands
(D) Carries the nation's burdens

**✅ Answer: (C)**
**📖 Explanation:** `Dict-` means to say or command. A dictator is one who says what must happen.
**Rule:** Root DICT.
**Difficulty:** Easy
**Company:** Accenture

**Q5. What is the antonym of "Apathy"?**
(A) Sympathy
(B) Empathy
(C) Passion
(D) All of the above

**✅ Answer: (D)**
**📖 Explanation:** `A-` (without) + `Path` (feeling) = without feeling. The opposite is having feeling (sympathy, empathy, passion).
**Rule:** Root PATH.
**Difficulty:** Hard
**Company:** Cognizant
