# The Ultimate 10-Second Shortcut Sheet

This sheet contains the absolute fastest hacks for Placement Aptitude. If you see these specific question patterns, **DO NOT** use algebra. Use these direct mapping tricks to solve them in under 10 seconds.

---

### 1. The "Successive Change" Trick (Percentages, Area, Population)
*   **Pattern:** Value increases by $A\%$ and then by $B\%$. (Or increases by $A$, decreases by $B$).
*   **Hack:** Net Change = $A + B + \frac{A \times B}{100}$
*   *(Note: Use negative signs for decreases. If area of a circle radius increases by 10%, apply $10+10+100/100 = 21\%$).*

### 2. The "Price vs Consumption" Trick
*   **Pattern:** Price of sugar increases by $R\%$. How much should consumption decrease to keep expenditure same?
*   **Hack:** Decrease % = $\frac{R}{100 + R} \times 100$
*   *(Note: If price DECREASES by $R\%$, use $100 - R$ in the denominator).*

### 3. The "False Weight" Trick (Profit & Loss)
*   **Pattern:** A shopkeeper sells goods at cost price but uses a 900g weight instead of 1kg. Find his profit %.
*   **Hack:** Profit % = $\frac{\text{Error}}{\text{True Value} - \text{Error}} \times 100 = \frac{100}{1000 - 100} \times 100 = \frac{100}{900} \times 100 = 11.11\%$

### 4. The "Buy X, Sell Y" Trick (Profit & Loss)
*   **Pattern:** Bought 6 apples for Rs. 5. Sold 5 apples for Rs. 6. Profit or Loss %?
*   **Hack:** Cross Multiply Method.
    *   Cost side: $6$ items, $5$ Rs $\implies 5 \times 5 = \mathbf{25}$ (This is your CP Base)
    *   Sell side: $5$ items, $6$ Rs $\implies 6 \times 6 = \mathbf{36}$ (This is your SP Base)
    *   Profit % = $\frac{36 - 25}{25} \times 100 = \frac{11}{25} \times 100 = 44\%$

### 5. The "Effective Rate" Trick (Compound Interest)
*   **Pattern:** Find CI for 2 years at 10%.
*   **Hack:** Don't use $P(1+R/100)^2$. Use Effective Rate = $2R + \frac{R^2}{100}$.
    *   $10\% \text{ for 2 yrs} \implies 20 + 1 = \mathbf{21\%}$.
    *   $5\% \text{ for 2 yrs} \implies 10 + 0.25 = \mathbf{10.25\%}$.
    *   Just calculate this $\%$ of the Principal.

### 6. The "Average Speed" Trick (Time, Speed, Distance)
*   **Pattern:** A man goes from A to B at $x$ kmph and returns at $y$ kmph.
*   **Hack:** Average Speed = $\frac{2xy}{x+y}$.
*   *(Warning: Do NOT just do $(x+y)/2$. That is a guaranteed wrong answer trap).*

### 7. The "Early/Late" Distance Trick (Time, Speed, Distance)
*   **Pattern:** If I travel at 30 kmph, I am 10 mins late. If at 40 kmph, I am 5 mins early. Find distance.
*   **Hack:** Distance = $\frac{S_1 \times S_2}{|S_1 - S_2|} \times \Delta T$
    *   $\Delta T = 15$ mins $= 15/60$ hours.
    *   $D = \frac{30 \times 40}{10} \times \frac{15}{60} = \frac{1200}{10} \times \frac{1}{4} = 120 \times \frac{1}{4} = 30$ km.

### 8. The "Platform vs Pole" Trick (Trains)
*   **Pattern:** Train crosses a pole in 10s and a 200m platform in 15s. Find train speed.
*   **Hack:** Speed = $\frac{\text{Length of Platform}}{\text{Extra Time taken}}$
    *   Speed = $200 / (15 - 10) = 200 / 5 = 40$ m/s.

### 9. The "N Times Time" Trick (Boats & Streams)
*   **Pattern:** A man takes 3 times as long to row Upstream as Downstream. Find Ratio of Boat to Stream speed.
*   **Hack:** Ratio $\frac{B}{S} = \frac{n+1}{n-1}$.
    *   $\frac{B}{S} = \frac{3+1}{3-1} = \frac{4}{2} = 2:1$.

### 10. The "LCM Work" Trick (Time & Work)
*   **Pattern:** A does work in 10 days, B in 15 days. Together?
*   **Hack:** LCM of $(10, 15) = \mathbf{30}$ (Total Work).
    *   A's efficiency = $30/10 = 3$.
    *   B's efficiency = $30/15 = 2$.
    *   Together = $30 / (3+2) = 30 / 5 = 6$ days.
    *   *(Never use fractions like $1/10 + 1/15$ again).*

### 11. The "At Least One" Trick (Probability)
*   **Pattern:** Probability of getting at least one Head in 4 coin tosses.
*   **Hack:** $1 - P(\text{None})$.
    *   $P(\text{None}) = P(\text{All Tails}) = 1/16$.
    *   Answer = $1 - 1/16 = \mathbf{15/16}$.

### 12. The "Handshakes" Trick (Permutation & Combination)
*   **Pattern:** 20 people in a room, everyone shakes hands with everyone else. Total handshakes?
*   **Hack:** $\frac{n(n-1)}{2}$.
    *   $(20 \times 19) / 2 = 190$.
    *   *(This also works for "Number of matches played in a tournament" and "Number of lines formed by $n$ points").*

### 13. The "Melting Ratio" Trick (Mensuration)
*   **Pattern:** A sphere of radius $R$ is melted into $n$ small spheres of radius $r$. Find $n$.
*   **Hack:** $n = (\frac{R}{r})^3$.
    *   If Big Radius = 10, Small Radius = 2.
    *   $n = (10/2)^3 = 5^3 = \mathbf{125}$.

### 14. The "Unit Digit" Trick (Number System)
*   **Pattern:** Find the unit digit of $3^{45}$.
*   **Hack:** Divide power by 4. Use remainder.
    *   $45 / 4 \implies \text{Remainder } 1$.
    *   Unit digit = $3^1 = \mathbf{3}$.

### 15. The "Difference HCF" Trick (LCM & HCF)
*   **Pattern:** Find the HCF of 48, 64, and 120.
*   **Hack:** HCF can NEVER be larger than the smallest difference between any two numbers.
    *   Difference between 64 and 48 = **16**.
    *   Does 16 divide 48, 64, and 120? (No, 120/16 fails).
    *   Factors of 16: 8, 4, 2.
    *   Does 8 divide all of them? Yes! $\implies$ HCF = **8**. (Zero prime factorization needed).
